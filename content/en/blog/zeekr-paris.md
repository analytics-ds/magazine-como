Searching for **Zeekr** in **Paris** quickly leads to a misleading impression: the brand displays a Paris address, several press articles mention a showroom in the capital, and yet nobody can name the street. The reality on the ground is more nuanced, and it deserves to be laid out clearly before booking an appointment or a test drive. Here is the exact state of the **Zeekr network in Île-de-France** as of 14 September 2026, taken from the brand's official dealer directory, along with the models, prices and expansion plans for the region.

## What Zeekr's Paris address actually covers

The Geely group's fully electric premium brand opened orders in France on 2 April 2026. Its commercial rollout moved fast, but unevenly across the country, and the Paris region illustrates that gap well.

The official directory that feeds the manufacturer's test drive booking form lists seventeen locations in France. One of them is called "Zeekr Paris et Ile de France". It carries no street number and no postcode, and the only service attached to it is test driving. Its geolocation falls on the Champ-de-Mars, a coordinate that acts as a Paris centroid rather than a real address.

In other words, this entry is an **administrative routing point** for test drive requests in the region, not a place anyone can visit. That is the nuance most press coverage lost along the way: launch communications mentioned dealerships in Aix-en-Provence, Paris, Lyon, Fréjus, Saint-Étienne and Rennes, and that "Paris" turned, through successive retellings, into a Paris showroom that nothing on the ground backs up.

One administrative detail keeps the confusion alive. ZEEKR FRANCE is indeed registered in Île-de-France, in Levallois-Perret in the Hauts-de-Seine, but that is the French subsidiary's head office, not a sales point open to the public.

## Where to test drive a Zeekr in Île-de-France

Two routes currently exist to get behind the wheel of a Zeekr in the region, and they do not serve the same purpose.

### The test drive booking tied to the Paris area

The first goes through the manufacturer's test drive form, which offers the "Zeekr Paris et Ile de France" entry. The request is logged, then an adviser arranges for a car to be made available. This route has the merit of existing without a long trip, but it does not allow anyone to see several models side by side or to talk to a salesperson in a showroom.

### Saint-Witz, the only physical site in the region

The second route is the only one that matches a genuine dealership in the traditional sense. The **Zeekr Roissy Saint-Witz** site, at 67 Grande Rue in Saint-Witz (95470) in the Val-d'Oise, is operated by the Bauer Paris group, already a representative for other brands. It is the only location in the region listed with the full set of services: vehicle display, test drives, delivery, pre-delivery inspection and after-sales service.

In practical terms, a Paris driver who wants to see the models in the metal, compare trim levels and leave with a written quote currently has to head north of the region, some thirty kilometres from Paris, right next to Roissy airport. That is the point most online information obscures.

## The Zeekr range available in Paris and its prices

Four models make up the French range, all fully electric and built on the Geely group's SEA platform, with an 800-volt architecture and a ten-year or 200,000-kilometre warranty.

| Model | Body style | Launch price |
|---|---|---|
| Zeekr X | urban SUV | from €37,990 |
| Zeekr 7GT | sports wagon | €45,990 |
| Zeekr 7X | family SUV | €52,990 |
| Zeekr 001 | premium shooting brake | €59,990 |

For city use in Paris, the **Zeekr X** is the obvious format: compact footprint, contained height and manoeuvrability suited to the capital's parking spaces. The [Zeekr 7X](/en/blog/zeekr-7x/) is aimed instead at families alternating between urban trips and longer journeys, while the 001 targets high-mileage drivers who value ride comfort.

One financial caveat is worth repeating: these vehicles are built in China and are therefore excluded from the French ecological bonus. Any price comparison against an eligible European model has to be made after the bonus, not on the list price. The full breakdown of the range and trim levels is covered in our feature on [Zeekr in France](/en/blog/zeekr-in-france/).

The 9X, a large 897-horsepower plug-in hybrid SUV unveiled in summer 2026, does not belong to this price grid: it is not sold in France at this stage.

## Buying a Zeekr in Paris: orders, delivery and used cars

New vehicle orders are placed through the brand's online configurator or with a dealer in the network. Delivery is then handled by the site covering the buyer's area, which for a Paris resident means Saint-Witz.

A secondary market is starting to appear alongside it. Classified platforms already list a handful of used **Zeekr 001** units located in Paris, priced between 60,000 and 67,000 euros depending on trim and mileage. These cars come from the first delivery waves of spring 2026. On such a recent brand, caution is warranted: check the service history and confirm that the official network will honour the remaining manufacturer warranty before signing.

For anyone still weighing the group's premium brand against its mainstream offer, the comparison with a [Geely dealer](/en/blog/geely-dealer/) is instructive. Geely Auto has a far denser network in the region, with sites in Pontoise, Vélizy, Colombes, Fontenay-sous-Bois, Stains, Port-Marly and in the 12th and 17th arrondissements of Paris, all run by the Como group, whose [Geely line-up in Île-de-France](https://como.fr/pages/l-univers-geely-par-como) covers the brand's full range. A buyer looking for a Chinese electric car available right now close to home will therefore find it more easily on that side.

## Charging a Zeekr in Paris: what the city changes

The most relevant technical argument for Paris use lies in the SEA platform's 800-volt architecture. It allows high rapid-charging power, around 30 minutes to go from 10 to 80 percent on the 001 according to the manufacturer, provided a charger capable of delivering that power can be found.

This is exactly where Paris imposes its constraints. Rapid DC charging remains concentrated on the outskirts, in retail car parks and along motorway corridors. In the city centre, the dominant offer is still the kerbside AC charger, often capped at 7 or 22 kilowatts, where the 800-volt advantage simply does not show. A resident without private parking therefore has to plan around slow overnight charging rather than quick top-ups.

The budget also deserves some forethought, because the gap between home charging and a session on a public rapid charger is considerable. Our analysis of the [electric car charging price](/en/blog/electric-car-charging-price/) sets out those orders of magnitude.

One final favourable point: Paris and the metropolitan area enforce a low-emission zone from which fully electric vehicles are exempt, with the corresponding Crit'Air sticker. On that front, a Zeekr enjoys the same treatment as any European electric car.

## The Zeekr network in the region is set to grow

The picture taken in mid-September 2026 is that of a network under construction, not a settled one. Several public data points confirm it.

Le Journal de l'Automobile published the first names of distributors selected by the brand in May 2026, among them the Como group, described as one of the largest smart distributors in France, with a sales point announced in the Val-d'Oise. A second article in the same outlet, in July 2026, refined that location to Pontoise, alongside the Bauer Paris group in Roissy. These announcements are distribution contract signings: as of this article's publication date, the brand's official directory does not yet list that sales point as open. A phone call ahead of any visit therefore remains essential.

The requirements placed on investors give a sense of the expected format. The brand asks each of them to open at least two sales points, with a showroom of no less than 200 square metres and a dedicated salesperson. This is not a shared corner inside a multi-brand dealership.

Finally, the announced trajectory is ambitious: 40 sales points in France by the end of 2026, 70 by the end of 2027, with new distributor names to be communicated at the Paris Motor Show. The Paris region, France's largest car market, is logically at the heart of that rollout. It is therefore reasonable to expect the situation described here to change in the coming months, and to check the official directory again before planning a visit.
