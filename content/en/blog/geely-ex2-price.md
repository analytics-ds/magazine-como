The **Geely EX2** is an electric city car positioned in the B segment, offering excellent value for money and equipment. Starting at **20,990 €**, it ranks among the most affordable electric cars on the French market, without sacrificing technology and comfort. With three trim levels offered, the **Geely EX2** appeals to all budgets and usage needs.

> **At a glance:**
>
> - **Starting price**: 20,990 € for the Pro version (excluding bonus)
> - **Three versions**: Pro, Max and Ultra, respectively at 20,990 €, 23,490 € and 25,490 €
> - **LFP battery**: reliable and high-performance technology with 35.4 kWh and 47 kWh depending on versions
> - **Range**: between 225 and 325 km on WLTP, up to 320 km in real-world use
> - **Flexible financing**: LOA, LLD, trade-in available to facilitate access to electric mobility

## Pricing of the three versions {#pricing}

The **Geely EX2** offers three commercial variants in France, designed to cover all customer segments. Here is the detail of prices and what they include.

| Version | Price excl. VAT | Battery | Power | WLTP Range |
|---------|---------|----------|-----------|----------------|
| **Pro** | 20,990 € | 35.4 kWh | 78-82 hp | ~225 km |
| **Max** | 23,490 € | 47 kWh | 100 hp | ~275 km |
| **Ultra** | 25,490 € | 47 kWh | 114 hp | ~325 km |

These prices are stated excluding the ecological bonus. The Pro version represents the ideal entry point for those looking to switch to electric without massive investment. The Max and Ultra show reasonable price gaps for substantial gains in range and power.

## Differences between versions {#differences}

### Pro version: access to electric

The **Pro** version starts at **20,990 €** and provides the essentials for urban and suburban mobility. It integrates a 35.4 kWh LFP battery, a motor of 78 to 82 hp and displays a WLTP range of approximately 225 km. It is the ideal configuration for regular commutes or small family trips in the city.

### Max version: the balance

At 23,490 €, the **Max** significantly raises comfort and capabilities. Its 47 kWh LFP battery offers approximately 275 km of range, while power rises to 100 hp. On-board equipment is enriched: larger touchscreen, better sound system, more refined interior finish, more comfortable seats.

### Ultra version: the pinnacle

At 25,490 €, the **Ultra** embodies the top of the line for this city car. It benefits from the same 47 kWh battery, but its engine reaches 114 hp (approximately 83 kW), guaranteeing more dynamic acceleration (0-100 km/h in 9.2 s). Technological equipment is maximal: high-end infotainment system, advanced driving assistance, premium upholstery.

## Ecological bonus and aids {#bonus}

The displayed price does not include the ecological bonus and other government aids. Although the Geely EX2 is manufactured in China, eligibility for aids depends on strict criteria: environmental score of the vehicle, battery, buyer category (individual, business) and household income. According to L'Argus, the conditions for access to the bonus are tightening each year (L'Argus, 2026).

The interested buyer should contact their dealer to precisely understand the amounts applicable at the time of purchase, as these aid schemes may fluctuate according to current legislation.

## Financing and purchase solutions {#financing}

Several paths open to the buyer to finance their Geely EX2:

**Cash purchase**: the most straightforward solution, with no interest or credit fees.

**Long-term rental (LLD)**: the customer rents the vehicle for a defined period (usually 3 to 5 years) in exchange for a fixed monthly payment. At the end of the contract, they return the car with no repurchase fees.

**Rental with purchase option (LOA)**: similar to LLD, but the buyer retains the option to purchase the vehicle at the end of the contract according to a residual value defined in advance.

**Bank loan**: a loan from a bank or automotive credit institution to finance the remaining amount.

**Trade-in exchange**: the dealer takes back the buyer's old vehicle and deducts its value from the Geely EX2 price, thus reducing the amount to be financed.

Como offers a range of solutions tailored to each situation. The support team will weigh the advantages and constraints of each option to guide the customer towards the optimal choice.
