The Geely Starray EM-i has been on sale in France since 29 April 2026, and its advertised price ranges from 29,190 € to 38,990 € depending on where it is ordered. Almost 10,000 € of spread on the same car.

That gap has nothing to do with haggling. It comes from the channel: four parallel routes now distribute this plug-in hybrid SUV in France, with pricing, lead-time and warranty rules that have little in common.

> **In short:**
> 1. **Buying a Geely Starray in France** runs through four channels: the **approved network** at the importer's price of **34,990 € to 38,990 €**, the **broker** from **30,946 €** (20 % off), the **demonstration vehicle** from **29,190 €**, and **leasing at 299 € a month**
> 2. **Geely Auto France sells neither directly nor online**: every order goes through an independent distributor, with seven groups signed by late April 2026, including **Como** and its **8 announced dealerships in Greater Paris**
> 3. Classified platforms listed **54 Starrays with under 10 km** on the clock in August 2026, at an average price of **33,568 €**: demonstration cars rather than used ones, available with no allocation wait
> 4. The channel changes neither the **8-year or 200,000 km warranty**, nor the **24-hour parts supply**, nor the exclusion from the **ecological bonus** that applies to plug-in hybrids

## The four purchase channels compared {#channels}

Working out **where to buy a Geely Starray** means comparing four routes to market, not ranking dealerships against each other. The table below sets out the conditions recorded on each of them in August 2026.

| Criterion | Approved dealership | Broker | Demonstration vehicle | Leasing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Entry price | 34,990 € (Pro) | 30,946 € | 29,190 € | 299 € a month |
| Discount off list | none, importer pricing | around 20 % | 20 % to 27 % | not applicable |
| Lead time | factory allocation | allocation plus transport | immediate, the car exists | subject to lessor stock |
| Choice of trim and colour | full, built to order | full, built to order | limited to available stock | limited to the offer range |

None of these four channels beats the other three outright. The approved network secures after-sales, the demonstration vehicle compresses both price and lead time, the broker opens up a discount on a car built to order, and leasing crushes the monthly cost. The decision rests on two variables only: tolerance for waiting, and the need for a workshop nearby.

## The approved network, the only channel at importer pricing {#network}

Geely Auto France chose neither direct sales nor online sales. The brand relies on independent distributors that buy cars from the importer and resell them under their own banner, a conventional model that several Chinese carmakers arriving before it had deliberately dropped.

Seven groups had signed by late April 2026, at the commercial launch. Como covers Greater Paris with eight announced dealerships, Maurin seven, BYmyCAR six between the Lyon area and the Alpes-Maritimes, Auto Réal Group three in the South West, Baldassari three along the Mediterranean coast, Elypse Autos three in the Grand Est, and Select Auto one in La Rochelle.

Prices are not negotiable within this channel. The importer sets the grid and distributors apply it, which is why a quote issued in Bordeaux shows the same figure as a Parisian one.

| Trim | List price | WLTP electric range | What the level adds |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pro | 34,990 € | up to 136 km | entry specification, already complete according to the specialist press |
| Max | 36,990 € | up to 136 km | mid-range level |
| Max+ | 38,990 € | 136 km homologated, 150 km measured by L'Argus | version tested by the press |

What can be negotiated is the trade-in value of the outgoing car and the accessories. The comparison of the [best Geely Starray dealer](/en/blog/geely-starray-dealer-france/) breaks down that room for manoeuvre distributor by distributor.

The network is still young, and the speed at which it is built governs access to the model. "Geely Auto wants to sell 6,000 cars as early as 2026 with a network of 70 dealers," L'Argus reported in 2026, against around thirty sales points open in June and 50 targeted for September. A buyer outside the covered areas therefore orders remotely, or waits.

## The 54 Starrays under 10 km that undercut list price {#zero-km}

The least known channel is also the most aggressive on price. In August 2026, classified platforms listed 54 Geely Starrays priced between 29,190 € and 39,840 €, at an average of 33,568 €.

Mileage tells the real story of those cars: almost all of them show 10 km or less, and the most used of the batch tops out at 937 km. These are not used cars but demonstration vehicles and tactical registrations, pushed back onto the market by distributors that need to hit their volumes.

Two examples give the measure of the gap. A Max+, the top trim at 38,990 € on the price list, appeared at 29,190 € with 2 km on the clock in the Bas-Rhin, 27 % below list. A Pro at 29,900 € showed 937 km in the Yvelines.

The catch sits on the warranty line, and it is easy to miss. The 8 years or 200,000 km run from first registration, not from the moment the keys reach the second owner. A car registered in May 2026 and delivered in September has already used up four months of cover, and its first workshop visit arrives that much sooner. Over three to four years of ownership the impact stays marginal against 9,000 € of savings. Over a long ownership period, it needs counting.

## The broker: 20 % off, one contact fewer {#broker}

Brokers position the Starray EM-i from 30,946 €, an advertised discount of 20 % off list price. The mechanism matches any other brand distributed in France: the intermediary buys in volume, sometimes on another European market, and passes back part of its margin.

This channel keeps the advantage of a new car built to order, with the chosen trim and colour, where a demonstration vehicle imposes whatever sits in stock. In exchange it adds a transport delay on top of the allocation delay, and above all it provides no assigned workshop.

That last point weighs heavily on a plug-in hybrid. The Starray EM-i pairs a 1.5-litre petrol engine with a high-voltage circuit, so two service programmes instead of one, where a purely electric [Geely E5](/en/blog/geely-e5-electric-suv/) keeps only a single one. Buying far from any approved site means handing those jobs to a workshop discovering them.

## Leasing at 299 € a month, with no purchase option {#leasing}

The launch long-term lease offer stands at 299 € a month including tax over 48 months and 40,000 km, after an initial payment of 3,500 €, on the Pro trim and until 30 September 2026. Metallic paint takes the monthly figure to 307 € on the same terms.

The full sum is worth writing down before comparing that figure to a loan instalment: 3,500 € upfront plus 48 payments of 299 € comes to 17,852 € handed over for a car that goes back to the lessor at the end. A long-term lease includes no purchase option, unlike a lease-purchase agreement.

This channel answers a specific need: driving a recent plug-in hybrid without tying up 35,000 € or carrying the resale risk on a brand that reached the French market only four months ago. That is a serious argument on a model whose three-year residual value remains unknown.

## Where the network actually operates, region by region {#geography}

Geographic coverage is the first filter, ahead of price. The network announced around thirty sales points in June 2026, 50 in September, 70 by year end, then 120 in 2027 and 170 in 2028.

In Greater Paris, Como lists five open Geely addresses out of the eight announced: Vélizy, Colombes, Paris Bastille, Fontenay-sous-Bois and Pontoise. The group remains the region's only Geely distributor, which also gives it the only demonstration fleet reachable without leaving the Paris basin.

In Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes, BYmyCAR runs four dealerships around Lyon, a tight mesh over a short radius. In the South West, Auto Réal Group covers Bordeaux, Toulouse and Perpignan, three addresses 250 km apart, a distance that changes the nature of any after-sales commitment. Along the Mediterranean, Baldassari holds Marseille, Toulon and Fréjus, with BYmyCAR adding two sites in the Alpes-Maritimes. In the Grand Est, Elypse Autos operates Mulhouse, Belfort and Épinal, a group that already distributes Volvo, Polestar and Lynk & Co, three brands owned by the same shareholder as Geely.

Blank areas were still numerous in August 2026, particularly Brittany, Normandy and the Centre region. The full network picture appears in the guide to [buying a Geely in France](/en/blog/buy-a-geely-in-france/), across all model ranges.

## What the channel does not change {#warranty}

Three elements do not depend on where the car is bought, and it helps to take them out of the debate.

The manufacturer warranty, announced at up to 8 years or 200,000 km, follows the vehicle rather than the seller. It applies across the approved network, including on a car bought from a broker, provided servicing follows the book. Geely also advertises spare parts within 24 hours, a commitment worth verifying with the assigned site rather than on a brochure.

The tax treatment is identical everywhere. The Starray EM-i does not qualify for the ecological bonus, which is reserved for fully electric passenger cars, and a plug-in hybrid is excluded by design. It does escape the CO2 malus thanks to its low homologated emissions, and its Crit'Air 1 rating grants access to low-emission zones.

The technical data, finally, does not move: 262 hp from a 1.5-litre engine paired with an electric motor, up to 136 km on electricity and a claimed 1,055 km of total range. Powertrain details are covered in the [Geely Starray EM-i price and range](/en/blog/geely-starray/) fact file.

## Four checks before signing {#checks}

**The first registration date.** On any car advertised with mileage, even 2 km, it sets the starting point of both the warranty and the service book. Ask for it in writing before paying a deposit.

**The assigned workshop.** Several Geely addresses open as showrooms first, with the workshop following some months later, which sends servicing to another site in the group. On a plug-in hybrid, the question is not only distance but certification for work on high-voltage circuits.

**A firm lead time, written into the order.** A factory allocation delay and a broker's transport delay are not quoted the same way. Insist on a date rather than a range.

**What the discount actually covers.** A 20 % discount advertised off list price compares trim for trim, not across levels: a discounted Pro is not a discounted Max+. The 54 listings recorded in August 2026 mixed all three trims across a 10,650 € spread. The brand itself, its ranges and its French timetable are detailed in the [Geely in France](/en/blog/geely-in-france/) dossier.
