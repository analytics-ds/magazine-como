## The Zeekr X, the premium entry-level model {#presentation}

The **Zeekr X** is the entry model into the range of [Zeekr, the Geely group's premium electric brand](/en/blog/zeekr-in-france/). Launched in Europe as early as 2023 on the Dutch and Swedish markets, it arrives in France in 2026 with a clear positioning: offering the technology of the brand's higher models in an urban format and at a price below the €40,000 mark.

### A cousin of the Volvo EX30

A notable peculiarity: the **Zeekr X** shares its SEA platform (Sustainable Experience Architecture) with the Volvo EX30, one of the best-selling electric SUVs in Europe. Both brands belong to the same group, Geely Holding.

The Zeekr X stands apart with a slightly larger size (4.43 m versus 4.23 m), a more technology-driven interior presentation and more powerful motors at the top of the range.

### Three versions in the catalogue

The French **Zeekr X** range includes three levels:

- **Core RWD**: entry rear-wheel drive version, 49 kWh battery, 330 km WLTP
- **Long Range RWD**: rear-wheel drive, 61 kWh battery, 405 km WLTP
- **Privilege AWD**: all-wheel drive, 69 kWh battery, 415 km WLTP and 0 to 100 km/h in 3.8 seconds

Standard equipment is comprehensive from the Core version: connected navigation, adaptive cruise control, 360° cameras and powered tailgate.

## Range and charging {#range-charging}

### Up to 415 km of WLTP range

The range of the **Zeekr X** goes from **330 km** for the Core version to **415 km** for the Privilege AWD version, in the mixed WLTP cycle. The Long Range rear-wheel drive version, certified at 405 km, represents the best range-to-price compromise in the line-up.

These values place the model in the upper average of the urban electric SUV segment, without however reaching the records of the brand's larger models.

### Fast charging for the segment

The **Zeekr X** accepts DC charging power of up to 230 kW depending on the version. Going from 10 to 80% battery takes around **18 minutes** on a suitable charger.

> "The SUV is credited with a WLTP range of up to 415 km in its Privilege all-wheel drive version, and 10 to 80% charging in 18 minutes."
> — La Tribune Auto, Zeekr X test drive in France, 2026

By comparison, a Peugeot e-2008 needs around 30 minutes for the same operation, and a Volvo EX30 around 27 minutes.

## Prices and versions available in France {#prices-versions}

### 2026 price list

The **price of the Zeekr X** in France breaks down as follows:

| Version | Drivetrain | WLTP range | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| X Core | RWD | 330 km | €37,990 |
| X Long Range | RWD | 405 km | €42,990 |
| X Privilege | AWD | 415 km | €47,990 |

Orders have been open since April 2, 2026. The first French deliveries took place in **May 2026**, at the same time as those of the [Zeekr 7X family SUV](/en/blog/zeekr-7x/).

### No French EV bonus

Built in China, the **Zeekr X** is not eligible for the French ecological bonus. At €37,990 without subsidy, it faces European competitors that can benefit from it. The brand compensates with a **10-year or 200,000 km** warranty and very comprehensive standard equipment.
