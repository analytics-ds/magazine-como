The **Mercedes A-Class** remains one of the most sought-after premium compacts in France. Whether you are buying new, used or leasing, several channels let you **buy a Mercedes A-Class** on good terms. This guide reviews the options, the prices per channel and the criteria to know in order to pick the **Mercedes dealer** that fits your needs.

## The Mercedes A-Class in 2026: models, engines and list prices {#a-class-2026}

The current generation of the **Mercedes A-Class (W177)**, on sale since 2018, offers a full range of petrol, diesel and plug-in hybrid powertrains.

In petrol, the A 180 (136 hp) starts at €42,295, while the A 200 (163 hp) and the A 250 (224 hp) climb to €44,950 and €50,200 respectively. In diesel, the A 180 d (116 hp) is available from €43,795.

For drivers watching their carbon footprint, the **plug-in hybrid A-Class** A 250 e delivers up to 60 km of electric range on the combined cycle, from €47,500.

The range comes in three main trim levels: Style, **AMG Line** and AMG Line Advanced. The **MBUX** infotainment system, with its touchscreen and voice control, is standard across the range.

The A-Class is reaching the end of its cycle, with **Mercedes-Benz** having announced a restructuring of its compact range. That makes this a strategic moment to secure favourable terms before the transition to the new models.

## The 5 channels for buying a Mercedes A-Class {#buying-channels}

### Buying from an authorised Mercedes-Benz dealer

The **authorised dealer** is the safest channel to **buy a Mercedes A-Class**. It provides the **manufacturer warranty** (2 years on a new car, extendable to 4 or 5 years), certified aftersales service and the option to test drive the car before buying.

The **Mercedes France** network counts more than 150 authorised outlets across the country. Among the best established **Mercedes dealers in France**, **Como Mercedes Paris** runs 16 dealerships in the Paris region and sells more than 8,500 vehicles a year, with permanent stock of new and used A-Class models.

The other advantage of an authorised dealer is direct access to official finance offers through **Mercedes-Benz Financial Services**, with negotiated rates and flexible packages (conventional loan, leasing with purchase option, contract hire).

### Going through an import broker

**Import brokers** such as PromoNeuve, Elite Auto or Caroom offer discounts of 15% to 25% on the list price of a new A-Class. The principle is simple: the broker buys the car in a European country where it is cheaper, then resells it in France.

The trade-off is the absence of dedicated aftersales support, no test drive before purchase and sometimes long delivery times (3 to 6 months). Servicing and the manufacturer warranty remain valid within the authorised network, but the customer relationship is less personal.

### Buying on an online used car platform

La Centrale, AutoScout24, L'Argus and Spoticar list several thousand **used Mercedes A-Class** ads in France. These platforms make it easy to compare prices, mileage and equipment.

The average price of a used **A-Class W177** (2019-2022) sits between €20,000 and €28,000 depending on the engine and overall condition. Older models (W176, 2012-2018) can be found from €12,000.

Caution is required: check the service history, the origin of the car and the condition of the MBUX system. It is better to favour trade listings carrying the **Mercedes certified used** label.

### Buying from a private seller

Buying privately generally delivers the lowest prices, with savings of 10% to 15% compared with the trade market. Leboncoin and ParuVendu carry most of the listings.

On the other hand, no legal conformity warranty applies (unlike a purchase from a professional). It is strongly recommended to have the car inspected by an independent mechanic and to check the outstanding finance certificate before any transaction.

### Choosing leasing

Leasing with a purchase option (**Mercedes PCP-style leasing**) and contract hire let you drive an A-Class without tying up significant capital. On a lease, monthly payments for an A 180 sit around €350 to €450 over 36 to 48 months, with an initial deposit of €3,000 to €5,000.

Leasing with a purchase option includes a buyout at the end of the contract, unlike contract hire where the car is handed back. To compare leasing terms in the capital, see our guide to the [best Mercedes leasing dealer in Paris](/en/blog/best-mercedes-leasing-dealer-paris/).

## How to choose your Mercedes dealer in France {#choosing-a-dealer}

Choosing the **dealer** is decisive for the quality of the buying experience on a **premium vehicle**. Several criteria deserve attention.

Geographic proximity makes visits, test drives and aftersales follow-up easier. A Mercedes dealer with large stock offers more choice in trims, colours and equipment, which shortens delivery times.

Reputation and online reviews are reliable indicators. Checking Google ratings, feedback on specialist forums and testimonials on social media gives a precise idea of service quality.

The services on offer also make a difference: part-exchange of your current car, tailored **Mercedes financing**, extended warranty, courtesy car during **Mercedes servicing**.
