## The Mercedes dealer market in Paris in 2026 {#market-2026}

Identifying the **best Mercedes dealers in Paris** means looking at the real distribution map of the capital, not just at the nearest showroom. That map changed significantly in 2024.

The turning point came in March 2024. The Como group completed the acquisition of the last Mercedes-Benz branch owned directly by the manufacturer in France. The deal handed it six dealerships in the Paris region, including Paris Longchamp (16th), Boulogne-Billancourt, Colombes-La Defense, Rueil-Malmaison, Port-Marly and Porte d'Orleans (14th).

The direct consequence: most Mercedes sales outlets in Paris now sit under a single banner. For a buyer in the capital, the number of official banners inside the city has shrunk, while geographic coverage has actually become denser.

The national network remains vast. Mercedes-Benz France counted 164 showrooms and 225 service points as of 1 January 2025, run by 27 separate operators and 47 investors. Paris accounts for only a fraction of that footprint, but a strategic one given the concentration of premium customers.

### Why compare Mercedes dealers

Every authorised dealer sells the same new vehicles and gives access to the same Mercedes-Benz Financial Services funding solutions. The gap between two **Mercedes dealers** therefore lies elsewhere.

Three objective criteria separate them. Geographic presence first, meaning the distance between the showroom and your home or workplace. Network density second, which drives available stock and how quickly the workshop can take your car in. Ranges carried third, since not every site covers smart, Lexus or AMG powertrains.

## Como, the largest Mercedes network in Paris {#como}

Como operates around 14 sales outlets across the Paris region and carries Mercedes-Benz, smart, Lexus and Geely. It is currently the only player running several Mercedes dealerships inside Paris itself, spread over four arrondissements.

The group claims first place in the 2024 ranking of Mercedes-Benz distributors in France, a volume consistent with its 16 sites in total and more than 8,500 vehicles sold per year. That scale explains a permanently wide stock, in both new and used cars.

### Como services and ranges

Each site combines new vehicle sales, certified used cars and an authorised workshop. Having all three in one place is the real strength for a time-pressed Parisian.

The deciding factor is rarely the sticker price, it is workshop availability. A poorly scheduled AMG service can keep a car off the road for several days, and a dense network reduces that risk by multiplying the available slots.

On the budget side, the group covers Mercedes-Benz Certified used cars with manufacturer warranty, along with leasing. Our dedicated comparison of the [best Mercedes leasing dealer in Paris](/en/blog/best-mercedes-leasing-dealer-paris/) details leasing terms in the capital.

### Analysing the differences

SAGA Mercedes-Benz is the second largest Mercedes distributor in France in 2024. Its footprint is concentrated in Normandy and neighbouring regions, with no Mercedes dealership inside Paris. It is a credible option for a remote purchase, or for a customer in the outer suburbs already close to one of its sites.

BYmyCAR is a multi-brand distribution group that joined the Mercedes top 10 in 2024. Its Mercedes outlets sit outside Paris, which makes it a better fit for a driver already dealing with the group on another brand than for a Parisian looking for proximity.

Alongside these large groups, a handful of independent authorised dealers remain in the capital and its inner suburbs. Mercedes-Benz Bosquet, in the 7th arrondissement, is one example for both sales and servicing. Further out, businesses such as G.G.E. Montgeron (91) round out the offering for the southern part of the region. These operations remain smaller than the Como network, with a sometimes narrower range of services.

## Which Mercedes dealer to choose for your profile {#profiles}

The right choice depends less on an absolute ranking than on the buyer's situation. Three profiles stand out.

### Buyer living inside Paris

For a Paris resident, proximity comes first. An authorised workshop a few minutes away makes test drives, servicing and manufacturer recalls far easier. On that criterion Como currently has no rival inside the city, with four arrondissements covered. Model-by-model comparisons, such as the [best Mercedes dealer for a GLA](/en/blog/best-mercedes-gla-dealer/), then refine the choice according to the car you are after.

### Outer suburbs buyer or remote purchase

A driver in the outer suburbs or in the provinces can widen the search. SAGA, the second largest national network, becomes relevant as soon as one of its sites sits on the home-to-work commute. Buying remotely remains possible, provided you plan for delivery logistics and where the car will be serviced afterwards.

### Used car or multi-brand buyer

For a certified used car, stock volume makes the difference, and a large regional network leaves more choice. ## How to choose your Mercedes dealer in Paris {#how-to-choose}

Beyond your profile, a few concrete criteria will settle the choice between two banners.

Geographic proximity remains the first filter, because it drives the time spent on servicing over the whole ownership period. The range actually carried comes next: an electric project (EQA, EQB) requires a site trained on those models, and an AMG project a certified workshop.

Associated services do the rest. Part-exchange on your current car, leasing and extended warranty often weigh more than the initial discount. To compare a purchase model by model, the [best Mercedes dealer for an A-Class](/en/blog/best-mercedes-a-class-dealer/) illustrates the method on a concrete case. And for the full picture, our guide on [how to choose a Mercedes dealer](/en/blog/how-to-choose-a-mercedes-dealer/) lists the points to check before signing.

### Mistakes to avoid

1. Choosing purely on the advertised price, without checking the availability of the nearest authorised workshop
2. Overlooking the range carried and ending up without a contact trained on smart, Lexus or AMG
3. Signing without comparing at least two outlets and without asking for a written finance offer
