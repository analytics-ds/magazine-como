The **Zeekr 007** is the electric sedan of the Geely group's premium brand, designed to rival the Tesla Model 3. With up to 870 km of range on the Chinese CLTC cycle, it is one of the longest-range electric sedans in the world. But be aware: the **007** is not sold in France. Its wagon derivative, the **7GT**, represents it on our market from €45,990. Here is what you need to know.

> **Key takeaways:**
> - The **Zeekr 007** is an electric fastback sedan rivalling the Tesla Model 3
> - Up to 870 km of CLTC range in China, roughly 700 km in WLTP equivalent
> - The 007 is **not sold in France**: the French range includes the X, 7GT, 7X and 001
> - Its wagon derivative, the **Zeekr 7GT**, is available in France from €45,990
> - First French deliveries of the 7GT scheduled from July 2026

## The Zeekr 007, the electric sedan with 870 km of range {#presentation}

The **Zeekr 007** was launched in China in early 2024 as the first sedan from [Zeekr, the Geely group's premium electric brand](/en/blog/zeekr-in-france/). Its stated goal: taking on the Tesla Model 3 on its own ground, that of the high-tech electric sedan.

The commercial bet paid off on its home market: the sedan recorded tens of thousands of orders within its first months on sale, driven by an aggressive value-for-money proposition.

### Record-breaking range

The main selling point of the **Zeekr 007** is its range. In its most efficient version, the sedan claims up to **870 km** on the Chinese CLTC certification cycle. As this cycle is more optimistic than the European WLTP, the equivalent sits at around **700 km WLTP**, which would place the model among the very best electric sedans in the world on this criterion.

## Why the Zeekr 007 is not sold in France {#availability-france}

The launch range of **Zeekr in France**, open for orders since April 2, 2026, includes four models:

| Model | Type | Starting price | Available in France |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zeekr X | Urban SUV | €37,990 | Yes |
| Zeekr 7GT | Sport wagon | €45,990 | Yes |
| Zeekr 7X | Family SUV | €52,990 | Yes |
| Zeekr 001 | Premium shooting brake | €59,990 | Yes |
| **Zeekr 007** | **Fastback sedan** | **not communicated** | **No** |

The **007** sedan was not selected for the European market. The reason is primarily commercial: in Europe, and particularly in France, the three-box sedan segment is declining in favour of SUVs and wagons. Zeekr therefore prioritised the **7GT**, the shooting brake derivative of the 007, better suited to European expectations.

### A strategy already seen at other carmakers

This approach is not unique. Several Chinese carmakers keep their sedans for the domestic market and only export their SUVs and wagons to Europe. The phenomenon is detailed in the [Chinese car brands guide](/en/blog/chinese-car-brands/) covering the French market.

## The Zeekr 7GT, the alternative available in France {#zeekr-7gt}

The **Zeekr 7GT** is the shooting brake derivative of the 007. It carries over its platform, powertrains and most of its technology, in a more versatile wagon body.

### What the 7GT offers in France

- **Starting price**: €45,990
- **Range**: around 615 km WLTP for the Long Range version
- **800-volt architecture**: ultra-fast charging from 10 to 80% in under 20 minutes
- **22 kW AC charger** as standard
- **Warranty**: 10 years or 200,000 km
- **First deliveries**: from July 2026

Positioned between the [Zeekr X compact SUV](/en/blog/zeekr-x/) and the 7X family SUV, the 7GT targets drivers who favour the road handling of a low wagon over the versatility of an SUV.

> "Zeekr plans to launch the 007 GT in Europe with a range of around 540 to 615 km WLTP depending on the version, at a starting price close to 46,000 euros."
> — Manufacturer data and European specialist press, 2026

## Zeekr 007 specifications and performance {#specifications}

For reference, here are the characteristics of the **Zeekr 007** sedan as sold in China:

| Specification | Zeekr 007 (China) |
|---|---|
| Type | Fastback sedan |
| CLTC range | up to 870 km |
| Estimated WLTP equivalent | around 700 km |
| Architecture | 800 volts |
| Drivetrain | RWD or AWD |
| 0 to 100 km/h | under 3 s (Performance version) |
| Direct competitors | Tesla Model 3, BMW i4, Hyundai Ioniq 6 |

The Performance all-wheel drive version of the 007 is among the fastest electric sedans on the Chinese market, with a claimed 0 to 100 km/h time under 3 seconds.

## The 007 and 7GT against the competition {#competitors}

On the French market, the **7GT** therefore carries the 007 sedan's colours against the competition:

| Model | Starting price | WLTP range | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Zeekr 7GT** | **€45,990** | **around 615 km** | **Shooting brake** |
| Tesla Model 3 | €39,990 | 702 km | Sedan |
| BMW i4 | €57,550 | 590 km | Fastback |
| Hyundai Ioniq 6 | €47,400 | 614 km | Sedan |
| Volkswagen ID.7 Tourer | €53,990 | 690 km | Wagon |

The Tesla Model 3 keeps the advantage on price and maximum range, but the **7GT** stands out with its wagon format, unique in this price segment, its ultra-fast 800-volt charging and its 10-year warranty.

For a more prestigious approach, the [Zeekr 001 shooting brake](/en/blog/zeekr-001/) remains the brand's high-end alternative, from €59,990.
