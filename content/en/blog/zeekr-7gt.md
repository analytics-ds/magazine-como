## The Zeekr 7GT, the range's sporty estate {#overview}

The **Zeekr 7GT** is a fully electric sporty shooting brake, derived from the Zeekr 007 saloon sold in China. It sits within the premium range of Zeekr, the electric brand of the Chinese Geely group, which also owns Volvo, Polestar and Lynk and Co, and co-owns smart with Mercedes-Benz. For background on the brand and its arrival, the [Zeekr in France](/en/blog/zeekr-in-france/) feature details the full line-up and launch schedule.

Positioned between the brand's SUVs and its saloon, the 7GT targets buyers looking for an elegant, high-performance and technological estate. It shares its platform and 800-volt architecture with the other Zeekr models, including the [Zeekr 7X](/en/blog/zeekr-7x/), the brand's family SUV.

## Design and dimensions {#design}

At 4,820 mm long, 1,910 mm wide and just 1,460 mm tall, the **Zeekr 7GT** shows the low, stretched proportions typical of a shooting brake. Its 2,900 mm wheelbase frees up generous rear space, while its 0.25 drag coefficient reflects careful aerodynamic work.

| Characteristic | Value |
|---|---|
| Length | 4,820 mm |
| Width | 1,910 mm |
| Height | 1,460 mm |
| Wheelbase | 2,900 mm |
| Boot | 456 litres |
| Front trunk (frunk) | 32 to 65 litres |
| Weight | 2,265 to 2,405 kg depending on version |
| Towing capacity | 1,600 kg |

The 456-litre boot remains modest for an estate of this length, a point raised by several road tests. The 7GT makes up for it with generous rear space and an electrically reclining rear bench backrest.

## Line-up, power and performance {#performance}

The **Zeekr 7GT** comes in three versions, two rear-wheel drive and one all-wheel drive.

| Version | Drivetrain | Power | Torque | 0 to 100 km/h | Top speed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Core RWD | Rear-wheel drive | 310 kW (421 hp) | 440 Nm | 5.3 s | 210 km/h |
| Long Range RWD | Rear-wheel drive | 310 kW (421 hp) | 440 Nm | 5.3 s | 210 km/h |
| Privilege AWD | All-wheel drive | 475 kW (646 hp) | 710 Nm | 3.3 s | 210 km/h |

Even in the entry version, the 7GT offers sports-saloon performance, with 421 horsepower and a 0 to 100 km/h in 5.3 seconds. The all-wheel-drive Privilege version raises the bar to 646 horsepower and 3.3 seconds, figures worthy of a sports car.

## Range and battery {#range}

The **Zeekr 7GT** offers two battery capacities depending on the version, in LFP or NMC chemistry.

| Version | Battery | Chemistry | WLTP range |
|---|---|---|---|
| Core RWD | 75 kWh | LFP | 519 km |
| Long Range RWD | 100 kWh | NMC | 655 km |
| Privilege AWD | 100 kWh | NMC | 558 km |

The Long Range RWD version is the most enduring with 655 km of WLTP range, while the all-wheel-drive version, more powerful and heavier, tops out at 558 km. To compare the 7GT with other market benchmarks, our comparison of the [best electric car](/en/blog/best-electric-car/) reviews models by segment and budget.

## Charging {#charging}

One of the **Zeekr 7GT's** major strengths is its 800-volt architecture, still rare on the market. It allows DC fast-charging power up to 480 kW, enabling a 10 to 80% top-up in 13 to 16 minutes depending on the version. On alternating current, the onboard charger goes up to 22 kW, above the market average, for a full charge in 4.5 to 5.5 hours.

## Price and versions {#price}

In France, the **Zeekr 7GT** is offered in three levels. Prices are before the ecological bonus, which does not apply as the car is built in China.

| Version | Price |
|---|---|
| Core RWD | €45,990 |
| Long Range RWD Launch Edition | €50,990 |
| Privilege AWD Launch Edition | €57,490 |

## Facing the German premium estates {#competition}

The **Zeekr 7GT** directly targets European premium estates, starting with the Mercedes CLA Shooting Brake, while offering a much lower entry price. According to L'Argus, which tested the model in 2026, the 7GT has serious arguments against the German benchmarks, even if the latter retain some advantages in finish and handling (L'Argus, 2026). Road tests praise the price-to-features ratio, the charging speed and the perceived quality, while noting a modest boot volume and touchscreen-heavy ergonomics.

## Where to buy the Zeekr 7GT in Île-de-France {#where-to-buy}

In the Île-de-France region, **Como is the exclusive Zeekr dealer**. It sells the brand's entire line-up, including the 7GT, and handles test drives, orders, trade-ins and financing. Details and appointment booking are available from [Como's website](https://como.fr/).

Like the brand's other models, the 7GT comes with Zeekr's 10-year or 200,000 km manufacturer warranty, one of the longest on the market, which offsets the absence of an ecological bonus. For a full picture of the brand's pricing and financing, the [Zeekr 007](/en/blog/zeekr-007/) saloon that the 7GT is derived from usefully completes the comparison.
