## New A-Class price in 2026: €39,750 to €86,100 {#new-price}

The **A-Class price** starts at €39,750 in France, the list price of the A 180 136 hp in Star Edition or Edition 140 trim. The top of the price list reaches €86,100 with Mercedes-AMG versions.

> "The price of a new Mercedes-Benz A-Class ranges from €39,750 to €86,100."
> Source: L'Argus, new car price list, July 2026

Between those two figures, the range reads along two axes: the engine and the trim level. Four combustion engines and one plug-in hybrid make up the 2026 line-up, all fitted as standard with a dual-clutch automatic gearbox (7G-DCT on petrol, 8G-DCT on diesel and hybrid).

### Prices version by version

| Engine | Power | CO2 (WLTP) | 2026 list price |
|---|---|---|---|
| A 180 petrol | 136 hp | 131 to 134 g/km | €39,750 to €45,650 |
| A 180 d diesel | 116 hp | 132 g/km | €41,300 to €46,950 |
| A 200 petrol | 163 hp | 131 to 134 g/km | €42,000 to €47,650 |
| A 200 d diesel | 150 hp | 128 to 129 g/km | €43,300 to €48,950 |
| A 250 e plug-in hybrid | 163 + 109 hp | 53 g/km | €49,300 to €54,950 |
| Mercedes-AMG | 306 hp and above | not disclosed | up to €86,100 |

The step from the A 180 to the A 200 comes down to 27 hp and €2,250 in Progressive Line trim. On the road, the difference shows when pulling away uphill and when overtaking on the motorway, rarely anywhere else.

The A 250 e plug-in hybrid plays a different game. Its homologated 53 g/km of CO2 keeps it clear of the French ecological penalty that applies to the combustion versions, all rated between 128 and 134 g/km. On a company fleet, the annual tax saving absorbs part of the €7,300 premium over an A 200.

### What the AMG Line step costs

Trim level weighs almost as much as the engine in the final invoice. On an A 180 petrol, the price list steps up as follows: €39,750 in Star Edition, €40,000 in Progressive Line, €40,800 in Business Line, €42,300 in AMG Line, €45,650 in AMG Exclusive Design.

That is €5,900 between both ends, with the same engine. The AMG Line step costs €2,300 and brings cosmetics only: wheels, bumpers and upholstery. On a car meant for resale after three years, that gap is not fully recovered, but it clearly widens the pool of used-car buyers. AMG Exclusive Design remains hard to justify unless a well-valued trade-in is part of the deal.

## Hatchback or Saloon: a €100 gap at identical trim {#saloon}

The A-Class comes in two body styles: the five-door hatchback and the Saloon with a separate boot. The Saloon starts at €40,100 as an A 180 136 hp Progressive Line, against €40,000 for the hatchback in the same configuration. One hundred euros separate the two silhouettes at identical equipment level.

The headline gap is misleading: the hatchback goes down to €39,750 thanks to the Star Edition and Edition 140 series, absent from the Saloon price list. The real difference sits at the top, where the Saloon peaks at €64,600 with the 306 hp Mercedes-AMG A 35 4Matic, while the hatchback pushes to €86,100.

| Version | Five-door hatchback | Saloon |
|---|---|---|
| Entry level | €39,750 | €40,100 |
| A 180 Progressive Line | €40,000 | €40,100 |
| A 200 AMG Line | €44,300 | €44,400 |
| A 250 e Progressive Line | €49,300 | €49,700 |
| Top of range | €86,100 | €64,600 |

## Used A-Class price: from €15,000 to €33,000 {#used}

The used market halves the invoice, sometimes more. Three price tiers stand out depending on the generation.

| Generation | Years | Typical mileage | Observed price |
|---|---|---|---|
| W176 (third generation) | 2012 to 2018 | 100,000 to 150,000 km | from €15,000 |
| W177 (current generation) | 2018 to 2021 | 60,000 to 100,000 km | €20,000 to €25,000 |
| W177 facelift | 2022 to 2024 | 20,000 to 60,000 km | €25,000 to €33,000 |

A July 2026 listing illustrates the middle of the market: a 2022 A 180 d 116 hp Style Line with 42,800 km sells for €25,900. At an authorised distributor in the Paris region, used A-Class stock runs from €20,890 to €32,890, with manufacturer warranty and a verified service book.

The A 200 petrol remains the most sought-after version second hand, available from €23,000, first because it holds its value better than the small-capacity diesels. The maths flip beyond 25,000 km a year, the point where the A 180 d becomes cheaper to run again.

One trap to avoid: the W176 under €15,000. The first-generation 7G-DCT gearbox and its dual clutch, billed between €1,500 and €2,500 through the network, quickly turn a bargain into a money pit. A complete service history is worth more than a flattering odometer reading. ## Monthly cost on a lease {#leasing}

The **A-Class price** question increasingly comes down to euros per month. Mercedes-Benz long-term lease offers start at €350 per month with no deposit, for an A 180 Star Edition over 45,000 km. Online brokers advertise monthly payments around €312 on equivalent entry-level versions.

| Formula | Observed monthly payment | Deposit | What remains at the end |
|---|---|---|---|
| Long-term lease, 36 months, 45,000 km | from €350 | none | car handed back |
| Lease with purchase option, 36 months | €380 to €450 | €0 to €5,000 | purchase option, around 55% of the new price |
| Classic loan, 60 months | €620 to €700 | 10% advised | ownership of the car |

Three parameters drive the monthly payment: the deposit, the annual mileage and the buy-back value. A €5,000 deposit cuts around €130 per month over 36 months. Moving from 10,000 to 20,000 km a year adds €40 to €60.

Monthly payments are often easier to negotiate than the cash price, as detailed in our guide to [negotiating a Mercedes A-Class in Île-de-France](/en/blog/negotiate-mercedes-a-class-ile-de-france/). Twenty euros saved on a monthly payment adds up to €720 over 36 months.

## The five items that move the final price {#variations}

The list price is only a starting point. Five lines explain most of the gap between the advertised price and the final cheque.

1. **The commercial discount.** At an authorised dealership it ranges from 4 to 8% on an in-stock car, and exceeds 10% on an end-of-model-year or showroom vehicle. Online brokers advertise up to 25% on some versions, with an entry price recorded at €38,119 in July 2026, but with no valued trade-in and no local workshop.
2. **Options.** The Premium package, panoramic roof and 19-inch wheels easily add €3,000 to €5,000. The MBUX system, on the other hand, is standard across the range.
3. **The ecological penalty.** Combustion versions, homologated between 128 and 134 g/km of CO2, are subject to it. The A 250 e plug-in hybrid, at 53 g/km, is not.
4. **The trade-in.** Valued face to face and stackable with the discount, it often weighs more than an extra point of discount.
5. **Incidental fees.** Registration, marking, plates and accessories account for €300 to €800, best discussed at the end of the negotiation, once the price of the car itself is settled.

## Where to buy an A-Class at the best price in the Paris region {#where-to-buy}

In the Paris region, stock depth is what moves the price. A distributor with several A-Class cars available immediately can play its own sites against each other, whereas an isolated dealership has to place a factory order and loses all room for manoeuvre.

[Como](https://como.fr/pages/mercedes-benz-classe-a), the number one Mercedes-Benz distributor in France in the L'Argus 2024 ranking, runs 13 Mercedes dealerships across the Paris region, three of them inside Paris itself, for around 8,500 vehicles sold per year. Its used A-Class stock runs from €20,890 to €32,890 and financing goes through Mercedes-Benz Financial Services, which makes it possible to adjust deposit, monthly payments and mileage in the same appointment.

Choosing the point of sale matters as much as choosing the version: the selection criteria are set out in our comparison of the [best Mercedes A-Class dealer](/en/blog/best-mercedes-a-class-dealer/).

One last figure before signing: on an A 200 AMG Line invoiced at €44,300, a 6% discount represents €2,658, roughly the cost of stepping up from Progressive Line to AMG Line. In other words, the negotiation pays for the higher trim level.
