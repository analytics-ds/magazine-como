## Why the dealer matters in Paris {#why-it-matters}

Finding a **dealer for a new A-Class in Paris** is not simply about comparing three prices. The list price starts at €39,450 whoever the seller is, because Mercedes-Benz sets the base price. The real difference lies elsewhere: the distance between home and showroom, access to an authorised workshop, the type of financing and the actual room for negotiation.

In the capital, buying a **new Mercedes A-Class** goes through three distinct channels. The first is the authorised dealer located in Paris, which sells, finances and services in one place. The second is the authorised distributor in the suburbs, reliable but far from the centre. The third is the online import broker, which slashes prices but has no local network.

Buying a €40,000 car is a three to four year commitment. Over that period, workshop proximity and the quality of follow-up weigh as much as the discount obtained at signing. Before choosing, it helps to understand [where to buy a Mercedes A-Class](/en/blog/where-to-buy-mercedes-a-class/) according to your real needs.

## Criteria for choosing where to buy a new A-Class {#criteria}

### Proximity and outlets inside Paris

Proximity is the first criterion in Paris, where few residents have a second car for workshop visits. An outlet inside the city avoids 30 to 50 minute trips to the outer suburbs for every service or manufacturer recall. It is also what makes it possible to test drive the **new A-Class** before committing.

### Status: authorised dealer or import broker

An **authorised dealer** signs a distribution contract with Mercedes-Benz. It sells new cars sourced from the official network, applies the manufacturer warranty and runs certified workshops. An import broker is an intermediary: it buys in volume, often abroad or from overstock, then resells at a discount, with no authorised workshop of its own.

### Financing: leasing, contract hire and loan

Most **Mercedes A-Class** buyers use financing. Leasing with a purchase option and contract hire are the most common formats. At an authorised dealer, financing goes through Mercedes-Benz Financial Services, the brand's in-house bank. Understanding how [Mercedes leasing works in Paris](/en/blog/best-mercedes-leasing-dealer-paris/) helps compare monthly payments at equal budget.

### Aftersales, test drives and delivery

An official dealer delivers, registers and handles workshop follow-up in one place, with genuine parts and Mercedes-Benz trained technicians. The import broker delivers the car but outsources servicing to a third-party workshop, which lengthens lead times if a warranty problem arises. A prior test drive, straightforward at a dealership, becomes rare through an online platform.

## Como: the Mercedes dealer in the heart of Paris {#como}

[Como](https://como.fr) has distributed Mercedes-Benz for 69 years and runs 16 outlets across the Paris region, including 13 **Mercedes dealerships**. Three of them are inside Paris itself: Como Bastille (75012), Como Wagram (75017) and Como Longchamp (75016). It is the only one of the three players compared to offer a Mercedes address in the capital proper.

### Key characteristics

- **Network**: 13 Mercedes dealerships in the Paris region, the densest coverage in the capital area
- **Presence in Paris**: three addresses inside the city (Bastille 12th, Wagram 17th, Longchamp 16th), complemented by the inner suburbs (Boulogne, Colombes, Fontenay)
- **Volume**: around 8,500 vehicles sold per year, which guarantees large stock and constant turnover
- **A-Class range**: petrol (116 to 163 hp), diesel (116 to 150 hp) and 250e plug-in hybrid (218 hp, around 70 km of electric range)
- **Financing**: leasing, contract hire and conventional loan through Mercedes-Benz Financial Services, with servicing and extended warranty in a single monthly payment
- **Aftersales**: authorised workshops across the Paris region, genuine parts and Mercedes-Benz trained technicians

That density changes the buying experience. A buyer in the 12th arrondissement books a **new A-Class** test drive at Bastille, signs on site and has the car serviced within the same network, without leaving Paris. Financing through Mercedes-Benz Financial Services brings a concrete advantage: the A-Class resale value is estimated by the brand itself, which pushes lease payments down.

In its 2024 ranking of Mercedes-Benz distributors in France, L'Argus places Como at the top of the brand's groups. A useful benchmark for gauging the network beyond the sales pitch.

## SAGA RCM and online import brokers {#others}

### SAGA RCM: an authorised network in the suburbs

SAGA Mercedes-Benz has carried the brand since 1971 and operates 23 outlets between the Paris region and the north-west. The group is a fully fledged **authorised distributor**, with certified workshops and manufacturer financing. On that basis, it offers the same guarantees as an official dealer on new car quality and aftersales.

Its Paris-region footprint is concentrated in the suburbs, though: Montgeron, Les Ulis, Rambouillet, Viry-Chatillon, Dourdan. No address inside Paris itself. For a city-centre resident without a replacement car, every workshop visit means a trip out of town. The network remains relevant for a buyer in the southern or western part of the region, less so for a Parisian.

### Import brokers and online platforms

Aramisauto, Elite Auto and aggregation platforms list **new or low-mileage A-Class** cars with advertised discounts. In this segment, prices observed in early 2026 range from €41,700 to €53,900 depending on trim, with lease payments from €361 per month.

The budget advantage is real: the discount reaches 8% to 15%, or €3,000 to €6,000 on a €40,000 car. The limits are just as real. The car is often stored outside the region, a prior test drive is rare, and aftersales is handed to a partner workshop that did not sell the vehicle. This channel suits a buyer who knows exactly which trim they want and who prioritises the net price over support.

## New A-Class prices in Paris {#prices}

The starting price of a **new A-Class** is €39,450 in 2026, in base trim. List prices are set by the manufacturer and remain identical from one authorised dealer to another. The difference plays out in occasional discounts, financing offers and how the part-exchange is valued.

| Version | New price observed in 2026 |
|---------|------------------------|
| A-Class 180 petrol (136 hp) | from €39,450 |
| A-Class 200 and 180d | €42,000 to €45,000 |
| A-Class 250e plug-in hybrid (218 hp) | €46,000 to €49,000 |
| High trims and well-equipped saloon | up to €54,000 |

These ranges correspond to new stock observed at dealerships and Paris distributors in early 2026. The exact list price depends on the trim and options chosen. For a detailed analysis of pricing, our page on the [price of a Mercedes A-Class](/en/blog/mercedes-a-class-price/) covers each powertrain.

On a lease, A-Class monthly payments start around €360 to €400 depending on the deposit and contract length. At an authorised dealer, the resale value estimate produced by Mercedes-Benz Financial Services pushes those payments down. Import brokers go through third-party banks, without that expertise on the model's residual value.

Buying from a broker saves a few thousand euros at signing. But the day the A-Class goes into the workshop for an MBUX system reflash or a warranty job, the lack of a local contact in Paris is paid for in time and travel. For a car kept three to four years, that calculation deserves to be made before signing.
