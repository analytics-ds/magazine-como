## How much a Geely Coolray costs {#how-much}

In its markets, the **Geely Coolray** sits at the lower end of the urban SUV segment, the category of the Peugeot 2008 and Renault Captur. Its pricing strategy rests on a simple principle: offer high-trim equipment levels at an entry-level price.

That equation works in the countries where it is distributed, where it ranks among the best-selling Chinese models. It does not transfer to France, for lack of official distribution. No dealer in the network can order one, and the model appears on no French price list.

## Coolray prices by market {#prices-by-market}

Prices recorded in the markets where the model is officially sold give an order of magnitude:

| Market | Price observed | Approximate equivalent |
|---|---|---|
| Tunisia | from 72,800 DT | in the region of €21,000 to €22,000 |
| Algeria, export offers | from €14,990 excluding tax | pre-tax price, excluding registration |

These figures should be read with caution. Pre-tax prices advertised by export intermediaries include neither import taxation, nor on-the-road costs, nor homologation. Euro equivalents also depend on the exchange rate applied at the time of the transaction.

### A price list that varies widely by trim

The Coolray comes in several trim levels and engine options, from the 1.0 turbo of 136 hp to the 1.5 turbo of 181 hp, plus a plug-in hybrid version reserved for certain Asian markets. The price gap between entry-level and top trim commonly reaches 20 to 25% within a single market. Version details are covered in our [full Geely Coolray specifications](/en/blog/geely-coolray/).

## The Coolray used in France {#used-france}

This is the only realistic route to driving a Coolray in France. Classified platforms regularly list a few units, with prices ranging from **€19,990 to €23,000** depending on model year, mileage and engine.

These prices call for a warning. A B-segment SUV advertised around €20,000 used competes directly with recent European models that do have a network, a warranty and an established residual value. Three risk factors need to be considered:

1. **No manufacturer warranty applicable in France**, as the model is not homologated for the Western European market.
2. **Uncertain parts supply**, since the French network does not list this model in its parts catalogue.
3. **Difficult resale value**, with no official valuation guide or structured demand on the French market.

These reservations do not apply to models in the official range, which are covered by the manufacturer warranty and the network. The buying benchmarks detailed in our guide to [buying a Geely in France](/en/blog/buy-a-geely-in-france/) apply to the latter only.

## What importing really costs {#import-cost}

Importing a Coolray from outside the European Union pushes the bill well beyond the advertised price:

| Item | Indicative amount |
|---|---|
| Customs duty | 10% of vehicle value |
| Import VAT | 20% |
| Shipping and administrative fees | varies by origin |
| Individual vehicle approval (DREAL) | required if not EU homologated |
| CO2-based penalty | based on the vehicle's homologated emissions |

Individual vehicle approval is the most demanding step. It requires the vehicle to be inspected for compliance with European standards, with the risk that modifications are required, particularly on lighting and safety equipment. On a turbocharged petrol SUV, the CO2 penalty is a second significant item, as it is calculated on homologated emissions.

Combined, these costs can represent 35 to 40% of the initial purchase price, before counting administrative delays. That explains why the import route remains marginal and reserved for particular cases.
