## Two premium compacts head to head

The Mercedes A-Class and the BMW 1 Series have been fighting over the premium compact segment for several years. In 2026, both models offer very similar levels of trim, technology and comfort. Choosing between them means comparing point by point.

## Engines and performance

The A-Class offers a range of engines from the 1.3-litre turbo (A 180) to the 2.0-litre turbo (A 250). The BMW 1 Series answers with its 1.5-litre three-cylinder (118i) and its 2.0-litre four-cylinder (120i).

| Criterion | Mercedes A-Class 180 | BMW 118i |
|---|---|---|
| Power | 136 hp | 136 hp |
| Torque | 200 Nm | 230 Nm |
| 0 to 100 kph | 8.8 s | 8.5 s |
| Combined consumption | 5.6 L/100 km | 5.8 L/100 km |

## Equipment and technology

Mercedes' MBUX system remains a benchmark for user interface. The standard twin screen, voice control and augmented reality navigation put the A-Class ahead on this criterion.

The BMW 1 Series responds with iDrive 9, also very intuitive, and a neatly executed digital cockpit. Both cars offer smartphone connectivity, adaptive cruise control and advanced driver assistance as standard.

## Price and financing

In 2026, the [Mercedes A-Class starts at around €35,000](/en/blog/mercedes-a-class-price/), while the BMW 1 Series begins at €33,500. The gap narrows on higher trims.

Both manufacturers offer attractive [leasing deals](/en/blog/best-mercedes-leasing-dealer-paris/), with comparable monthly payments of around €350 to €400 per month.

## Verdict

Choosing between these two compacts depends on the buyer's priorities. The A-Class stands out for its onboard technology and premium cabin. The 1 Series offers more dynamic handling and a slightly more accessible entry price.
