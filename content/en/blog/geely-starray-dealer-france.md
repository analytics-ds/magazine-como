Price is useless as a way to pick a **Geely Starray dealer**. The importer sets the range from 34,990 € to 38,990 €, the launch lease rates are framed by a single finance provider, and no distributor controls either figure. What does vary sits in after-sales.

And on that ground, the Starray EM-i raises a question the other Geely models do not. It is a plug-in hybrid: it keeps a complete combustion engine and adds a traction battery on top. Two servicing schedules, two workshop skill sets, across a warranty advertised at up to 8 years.

## A plug-in hybrid needs two workshops, not one {#workshops}

The Starray EM-i powertrain pairs a 1.5-litre Atkinson-cycle engine with two electric motors, for a combined 262 hp and a battery good for 136 km of pure electric running. The manufacturer's GEA architecture drives three modes: Pure, Hybrid and Power.

That configuration doubles the workshop's scope. A mechanic working on a high-voltage circuit needs individual certification, a requirement set out in the French order of 20 November 2017 and articles R4141-13, R4141-14 and R4544-3 of the Labour Code. Levels run from B0L, one day of training for non-electrical work, up to B2VL, BRL and BCL, three days plus a refresher every three years for live work.

The same workshop has to keep its conventional combustion tooling. A Starray EM-i still needs oil changes, an oil filter, an air filter, spark plugs and a coolant circuit, exactly like a petrol SUV. A distributor coming from the fully electric side has not necessarily retained that chain.

The reverse holds too, and it is the more common case in 2026. A generalist group whose business has rested on combustion engines for thirty years masters the petrol half of the schedule without having trained its staff on high voltage. The question to ask at the dealership is a simple one: how many certified technicians work at this specific site, not across the group.

A third point rarely makes it into the conversation. A plug-in hybrid driven mostly on electricity runs its petrol engine very little, which creates issues specific to this powertrain: fuel ageing in the tank, brake pads seizing for lack of use, condensation in the exhaust. A workshop used to PHEVs recognises these symptoms and does not mistake them for a fault.

## What the Starray EM-i keeps in servicing that the E5 drops {#servicing}

The difference between the two SUVs in the French range reads more clearly item by item. The E5 is fully electric, the Starray EM-i is a plug-in hybrid, and their servicing schedules are not the same length.

| Servicing item | Geely Starray EM-i (plug-in hybrid) | Geely E5 (fully electric) |
|---|---|---|
| Oil change and oil filter | yes | no |
| Air filter, spark plugs, engine coolant circuit | yes | no |
| High-voltage circuit and traction battery | yes | yes |
| Electrical certification for the technician | required | required |
| Brake pads | wear slowed by regenerative braking | wear slowed by regenerative braking |
| Ecological bonus at purchase | outside the scheme by definition | 0 € despite being electric |

The bonus deserves a clarification, because the two models fail it for different reasons. The E5 is a genuine electric vehicle, but its Ademe environmental score sits below the 60 out of 80 threshold required in 2026. The Starray EM-i simply falls outside the scheme, which covers fully electric passenger cars only. The detail of that trade-off appears in our analysis of the [Geely EX5 dealer](/en/blog/geely-ex5-dealer-france/) question.

Regenerative braking works the other way and lightens the bill. By recovering deceleration energy, it puts less load on the pads, whose service life stretches noticeably compared with a combustion SUV of the same weight.

## Como, five Geely dealerships open out of eight announced {#como}

Como is the only Geely distributor based in the Greater Paris area. The group has announced eight addresses for the brand, but its website currently identifies only five: Vélizy, Colombes, Bastille, Fontenay-sous-Bois and Pontoise. The gap is unremarkable for a four-month-old network, it simply changes how a visit should be prepared.

Which brings the least obvious advice in this article: it is worth confirming that the intended address is among the five listed before driving there. An opening announced in the trade press sometimes corresponds to a filed permit rather than an open showroom with a workshop behind it.

The group [presents the Starray EM-i and its pricing for all of its centres](https://como.fr/pages/geely-starray-em-i). It claims forty years of activity, close to 500 staff and in the order of 8,500 vehicles delivered per year, across thirteen Mercedes-Benz dealerships, eight smart outlets, five Geely addresses and one Lexus site.

The smart portfolio is what matters most to a Starray buyer. Since 2019, smart has been a joint venture owned in equal shares by Mercedes-Benz Group and Geely Holding, and its recent models sit on an electric platform designed by Geely. A smart distributor therefore already services, without putting it that way, vehicles built on the engineering of the group that produces the Starray.

The combustion half of the equation comes on top. The Mercedes-Benz and Lexus ranges have included plug-in hybrids for several years, which implies workshops that hold both ends of the schedule, petrol engine and high voltage. That is exactly the combination a Starray EM-i calls for.

## Spare parts within 24 hours: the commitment to pin down {#parts}

After-sales for a brand that arrived in April 2026 rests on a logistics promise the manufacturer puts forward, relayed by its network:

> "Geely offers a warranty of up to 8 years or 200,000 km depending on the model, together with spare parts availability within 24 hours."
>
> Como, Geely distributor in the Greater Paris area, 2026

The commitment covers the importer's supply chain, not how long the car is off the road. A part delivered within 24 hours to a saturated workshop waits for its fitting slot like any other.

The wording also rewards close reading. "Up to 8 years or 200,000 km" and "depending on the model" are two reservations that prompt the same question at the dealership: which exact term applies to the Starray EM-i, and does the traction battery fall under the same cover as the rest of the car. The answer sits in the contract terms, not in the sales brochure.

Over a long ownership period, this matters more than the discount secured at signing. Eight years of warranty imply eight years of workshop visits, and every kilometre between home and the assigned site is paid at each service.

## The 299 € lease grants no purchase option {#finance}

The launch offer advertises 299 € per month including tax for a Starray EM-i Pro, over 48 months and 40,000 km, after an initial payment of 3,500 €. The arrangement is underwritten by Drivalia Lease France, restricted to private buyers, and runs until 30 September 2026.

This is a long-term lease, not a lease-purchase. The distinction is structural: at the end of the term the car goes back to the lessor and no purchase right opens up. Reconditioning charges and excess mileage fees may be added.

The monthly figure covers neither registration, nor insurance, nor servicing. The advertised 299 € is therefore a floor, on top of which sit items that two dealerships in the same network will not necessarily price the same way. Metallic paint, for its part, raises the figure to 307 € per month on the same terms.

Choosing a long-term lease is not a neutral move for a brand present on the French market for four months. Nobody yet knows the resale value of a Starray EM-i at four years old, and a long-term lease places that risk with the lessor rather than the driver. Pricing benchmarks for the whole range are set out in our review of [Geely prices in France](/en/blog/geely-france-price/). Leasing is only one of four possible routes: the overview of [where to buy a Geely Starray](/en/blog/where-to-buy-geely-starray/) sets the approved network, the broker, the demonstration vehicle and the lease against each other on both price and lead time.

## Trade-in, the only genuinely negotiable item {#tradein}

With list prices locked and lease rates framed by the importer, the commercial discussion shifts entirely onto the existing car. It is the only figure a salesperson can move.

A distributor that also runs a used-car volume values a trade-in better, because it knows what the incoming car will resell for. A network centred on a single brand and a recent new-car stock has less room on that item.

Two other lines stay open without carrying the same weight: the accessories included, and the installation of a home charge point, usually negotiated in the same file as the financing. A full charge takes around two hours on an 11 kW wallbox, against a whole night on a domestic socket.

One last figure is worth holding onto before signing: during a brand's first months of operation, a site fields one or two demonstration cars per model. Booking the test drive in advance, battery charged, is the only way to judge the Starray's Pure mode rather than its behaviour in hybrid, something the [Geely Starray EM-i specifications](/en/blog/geely-starray/) also set out.
