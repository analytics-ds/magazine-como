Seven retail groups share the Geely brand in France as of spring 2026. The largest, Como, runs eight outlets, all in the Greater Paris area. The smallest, Select Auto, has a single site in La Rochelle. Identifying the **best Geely dealer** therefore means comparing networks of very unequal size, for a brand whose model range and list prices are the same everywhere.

> **Key points:**
> - Seven groups distribute Geely in France: **Como** (8 sites), **Maurin** (7 announced), **Auto Réal Group**, **Baldassari Automobiles** and **Elypse Autos** (3 each), **BYmyCAR** (2) and **Select Auto** (1)
> - Como holds the densest coverage in the country, concentrated in **Greater Paris**, and also distributes Mercedes-Benz, smart and Lexus
> - Prices are not negotiated site by site: **€20,990** for the EX2, **€34,990** for the Starray EM-i, **€37,990** for the E5, with leasing rates of **€366 to €439 per month**
> - Geely requires every distributor to run an exclusive showroom of at least **250 sq m** with a dedicated salesperson
> - The real differentiator in 2026 is not the number of showrooms but whether the nearest site has a **workshop**

## Geely distributors ranked by network size {#ranking}

Geely Auto France sells nothing directly. The brand has handed its entire distribution to established automotive groups, confirmed in waves since late April 2026. Here is the current ranking, based on signed Geely sites:

| Rank | Retail group | Geely sites | Area covered | Other brands operated |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Como** | 8 | Greater Paris | Mercedes-Benz, smart, Lexus |
| 2 | **Maurin** | 7 announced for 2026 | cities not disclosed | multi-brand |
| 3 | **Auto Réal Group** | 3 | Bordeaux, Toulouse, Perpignan | multi-brand |
| 3 | **Baldassari Automobiles** | 3 | Marseille, Toulon, Fréjus | multi-brand |
| 3 | **Elypse Autos** | 3 | Mulhouse, Belfort, Épinal | Volvo, Polestar, Lynk & Co |
| 6 | **BYmyCAR** | 2 | Villeneuve-Loubet, Le Cannet | multi-brand |
| 7 | **Select Auto** | 1 | La Rochelle | multi-brand |

This ranking comes with a caveat worth stating upfront: none of these groups covers two regions at once. Comparing Como and Select Auto only makes sense as a measure of how solid a partner is, not as a way to settle a purchase. A buyer in La Rochelle has no reason to look towards the Paris region.

Network density does keep some practical value. On a brand still in launch phase, demonstration cars and production allocations are shared between the sites of a single group. Calling three Como dealerships often means comparing three different delivery lead times, something a single-site distributor cannot offer.

## What a Geely dealer must guarantee in 2026 {#guarantees}

The specification imposed on distributors is unusually strict for a brand just starting out. Thomas Léonard, commercial and network development director at Geely Auto France, sets out the first condition:

> "We ask distributors to open an exclusive showroom of at least 250 sq m and to assign a dedicated salesperson."
>
> Thomas Léonard, Geely Auto France, quoted by Journal Auto, April 2026

Three further commitments can be checked site by site. The manufacturer warranty covers 8 years or 200,000 km, backed by three years of roadside assistance, placing the brand above European standards in the segment. Spare parts run through a warehouse opened in northern France and a European hub, with a next-day delivery promise across the country. Sharing a site with BYD, MG or Chery is forbidden.

That exclusivity has a direct effect on opening schedules. Fitting out 250 sq m of dedicated space takes time, and several addresses start as display space before a workshop follows. This is where scepticism towards coverage announcements is healthy: an open showroom does not mean an available workshop, and for servicing, only the workshop address counts. The full list of outlets is set out in our overview of the [Geely dealer network](/en/blog/geely-dealer/).

## Como, the leading Geely network in Greater Paris {#como}

With eight signed sites, the Como group is ahead of every other distributor of the brand. The addresses cover Paris 12th and Paris 17th within the city, Colombes and Fontenay-sous-Bois in the inner suburbs, then Stains, Vélizy, Port Marly and Pontoise further out. No other group operates in the Paris region.

The distributor's profile matters as much as the number of addresses. Como operates Mercedes-Benz, smart and Lexus, with around 500 staff and in the region of 8,500 vehicles delivered per year. Reception standards, courtesy cars and workshop lead times applied to premium brands transfer mechanically to Geely, since the teams and the workshops are the same.

One detail matters for a Paris-area buyer in a hurry: the eight sites are spread across the four corners of the region, putting most communes within thirty minutes of an outlet. The [offers on new Geely vehicles](https://como.fr/pages/offres-neufs-geely) and test drive bookings run through the same channel for all eight addresses.

## The other groups distributing Geely in France {#other-groups}

The Maurin group is the most recent arrival, with seven Geely dealerships announced for 2026, which will place it second in the network. The cities involved have not been disclosed at this stage.

Elypse Autos has the most coherent technical profile. The group already operated Volvo, Polestar and Lynk & Co, three brands held by the same shareholder as Geely. Mechanic training, diagnostic tools and parts logistics partly rest on shared foundations, a real advantage across its three sites in Mulhouse, Belfort and Épinal.

Auto Réal Group holds the South West with Bordeaux, Toulouse and Perpignan, while Baldassari Automobiles covers Marseille, Toulon and Fréjus. On the Riviera, BYmyCAR adds Villeneuve-Loubet and Le Cannet, bringing the number of Geely sites between Bouches-du-Rhône and Alpes-Maritimes to five. Select Auto closes the ranking with its La Rochelle outlet.

Then there are the blank spots. Normandy, Brittany and Centre-Val de Loire had no confirmed distributor as of mid-2026. The brand's schedule calls for 50 outlets in September 2026 and 70 by year end, against around thirty at the end of June. For a buyer in those regions, waiting a few months costs less than 300 kilometres for every service.

## Test driving a Starray EM-i or an E5 before buying {#test-drive}

The French range comes down to three models, all available to test drive in store:

| Model | Type | Range | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Geely EX2** | electric city car | not disclosed | from €20,990 |
| **Geely Starray EM-i** | hybrid compact SUV, 262 hp | 136 km electric, 1,055 km total | €34,990 to €38,990 |
| **Geely E5** | electric compact SUV | 430 to 475 km | €37,990 to €41,990 |

A test drive carries more weight here than on an established brand. Perceived interior quality, the behaviour of the Starray EM-i's petrol generator and the ergonomics of the E5's 15.4-inch screen cannot be judged from a spec sheet. Our profile of the [Geely E5 electric SUV](/en/blog/geely-e5/) and of the [Geely Starray EM-i hybrid](/en/blog/geely-starray/) set out what to watch during the drive. The checks specific to a fully electric model, high-voltage workshop included, are covered separately in our comparison of [Geely EX5 dealers](/en/blog/geely-ex5-dealer-france/). Those that apply to a plug-in hybrid, combustion servicing included, appear in our comparison of [Geely Starray dealers](/en/blog/geely-starray-dealer-france/).

The number of demonstration cars allocated to each site drives the waiting time. Over the first months of operation, one or two units per model per dealership is the norm, which explains delays of several weeks at recently opened sites.

## Leasing and launch offers: what the dealer actually changes {#financing}

Monthly rates are negotiated by the importer, not by the distributor. Long-term leasing on the Starray EM-i runs from €366 to €419 per month with no deposit, on a 48-month, 40,000 km contract. The E5 sits between €399 and €439 per month. A launch offer at €299 per month circulated at the start, with a deposit of 10% on the Starray and 13% on the E5. Financing is arranged through CA Auto Bank, with Drivalia handling long-term leasing.

The dealer's room for manoeuvre lies elsewhere: the trade-in value of the old car, the accessories included and the delivery lead time. Those are the only three points where putting two dealerships in competition has a measurable effect. Full details of the packages appear in our article on [Geely prices and financing in France](/en/blog/geely-france-price/), and the complete process in our guide to [buying a Geely in France](/en/blog/buy-a-geely-in-france/).

## Which Geely dealer for which buyer {#profiles}

| Buyer's situation | Distributor to favour | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Living in Greater Paris | **Como** | only group in the region, 8 sites, premium culture |
| Provence and the Riviera | **Baldassari** or **BYmyCAR** | 5 sites combined, from Marseille to Le Cannet |
| South West | **Auto Réal Group** | Bordeaux, Toulouse, Perpignan |
| Grand Est | **Elypse Autos** | already a Volvo, Polestar and Lynk & Co distributor |
| Charente-Maritime | **Select Auto** | single site in La Rochelle |
| Normandy, Brittany, Centre-Val de Loire | wait for September or late 2026 | 50 then 70 sites announced |

One last figure to settle the question: Geely targeted 6,000 deliveries in France in its first year and 5% market share by 2030, roughly 80,000 vehicles in a market of 1.6 million units. By that date, 200 outlets are planned, against around sixty today. In most regions, the dealer chosen in 2026 will be the one whose mechanics were trained two or three years before everyone else's.
