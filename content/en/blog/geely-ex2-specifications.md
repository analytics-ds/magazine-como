The **Geely EX2** is the Geely group's fully electric city car, built around affordability, range and practicality. Based on the GEA platform (Global Intelligent New Energy Architecture), it combines a lithium iron phosphate (LFP) battery with a synchronous electric motor. Here are its detailed **specifications**, grouped by main category.

> **In brief:**
> - Length 4,135 mm, width 1,805 mm, wheelbase 2,650 mm
> - LFP battery in two capacities: around 35.4 kWh or around 47 kWh
> - WLTP range of roughly 225 to 325 km depending on version
> - Electric motor of 78 to 114 hp, torque of 130 to 150 Nm
> - DC fast charging up to 80 kW: 30 to 80% in 21 to 25 minutes

## Dimensions and volumes {#dimensions-and-volumes}

The **Geely EX2** adopts a versatile city car format, with a 2,650 mm wheelbase that optimises interior space for a contained length of 4.13 metres. The interior room is detailed in our dedicated analysis of the [Geely EX2 dimensions](/en/blog/geely-ex2-dimensions/).

| Characteristic | Value |
|---|---|
| Length | 4,135 mm |
| Width | 1,805 mm |
| Height | around 1,570 mm |
| Wheelbase | 2,650 mm |
| Kerb weight | 1,215 to 1,365 kg depending on version |
| Rear boot | 375 litres |
| Front trunk (frunk) | around 70 litres |

The **375-litre boot** handles everyday shopping and luggage, while the front trunk is ideal for storing charging cables.

## Battery and range {#battery-and-range}

The **Geely EX2** uses a **lithium iron phosphate** (LFP) battery, a cobalt-free chemistry known for its durability, thermal safety and resistance to fast charging cycles. Two capacities are offered depending on the version.

| Battery | Capacity | Indicative WLTP range |
|---|---|---|
| Small battery | around 35.4 kWh | lower end, around 225 km |
| Large battery | around 47 kWh | upper end, up to around 325 km |

The **real-world range** reported in early tests is between **280 and 320 km** in mixed use. Figures quoted under the older, more optimistic NEDC or CLTC cycles can reach around 395 km, but the WLTP cycle remains the reference for comparing vehicles in Europe. This topic is covered in our focus on the [Geely EX2 range](/en/blog/geely-ex2-range/).

## Powertrain and performance {#powertrain-and-performance}

The **Geely EX2** motor is a **permanent magnet synchronous** type. Two power levels are offered depending on the version.

| Characteristic | Entry version | High version |
|---|---|---|
| Power | around 58 to 60 kW (78 to 82 hp) | around 83 kW (114 hp) |
| Torque | 130 Nm | 150 Nm |
| 0 to 100 km/h | around 11.5 s | around 9.2 s |
| Top speed | 130 km/h | 130 km/h |

The **Geely EX2** does not claim a sporty character. Its positioning and urban vocation make it a pragmatic electric car, with performance that is sufficient for the city, the suburbs and expressway trips.

## Charging {#charging}

The **Geely EX2** supports two charging modes.

- **Alternating current (AC)**: up to 7.4 kW, for a full charge in about 5 hours, suited to overnight charging at home or at the office.
- **Direct current (DC)**: up to 80 kW fast charging, allowing a 30 to 80% top-up in 21 to 25 minutes.

As on most electric vehicles, charging speed slows sharply beyond 80% to protect the battery, so most fast-charging sessions stop at that level.

## Versions and prices {#versions-and-prices}

The **Geely EX2** is sold in France in three trims. Prices are before the ecological bonus.

| Version | Price (before bonus) |
|---|---|
| Pro | €20,990 |
| Max | €23,490 |
| Ultra | €25,490 |

The **Pro** version is the entry point, with the smaller battery and the entry-level motor. The **Max** and **Ultra** trims step up in equipment and battery capacity, which explains the price gap and the range gain.
