The **Zeekr 009** is the flagship of the Geely group's premium brand: a 5.21 m electric MPV with six individual seats, 545 hp and the first production 140 kWh battery on the market. This model, which claims up to 822 km of CLTC range, is not sold in Western Europe. Here are its detailed specifications, its equipment and its place within the Zeekr range.

> **Key points:**
> - The **Zeekr 009** is a 5.21 m six-seat luxury electric MPV
> - Two batteries: 116 kWh for 702 km CLTC, or **140 kWh for 822 km CLTC**
> - 545 hp and 686 Nm, 0 to 100 km/h in 4.5 s despite a 2,830 kg kerb weight
> - A 7.4 sq m interior: first-class style seats, 15.6-inch ceiling screen, 20 speakers
> - **Not sold in France**: the brand's European range is built around the X, 7X, 7GT, 001 and 007

## The 009, flagship of the Zeekr range {#overview}

The **Zeekr 009** occupies a distinctive place in electric vehicle production: that of the luxury MPV, a segment almost non-existent in Europe but highly prized in Asia for executive transport and premium family use.

Built on the Geely group's **SEA** platform, the same modular architecture underpinning the brand's entire range, it shares its technical foundations with the **Volvo EM90**, Volvo Cars belonging to the same shareholder. That kinship explains a level of finish and sound insulation claimed to European standards.

### Outsized dimensions

At 5,209 mm long, 2,024 mm wide and 1,848 mm tall with a **3,205 mm wheelbase**, the 009 comfortably exceeds the benchmarks of the large MPV segment. That exceptional wheelbase frees up a 7.4 sq m interior volume, allowing three rows of individual seats without compromising legroom.

A drag coefficient of **0.27** is remarkable for a body of this volume, where MPVs usually sit above 0.30. It is a direct contributor to the claimed range.

## Battery and range {#battery-range}

### Two capacities, one of them unprecedented

The 009 is offered with two batteries:

- **116 kWh** for up to **702 km** of CLTC range
- **140 kWh** for up to **822 km** of CLTC range

The 140 kWh version uses a **CTP 3.0 pack known as Kirin**, presented by the manufacturer as the first production battery of this capacity on a series vehicle. It places the 009 among the best-endowed electric vehicles on the market in terms of onboard energy.

### Reading CLTC figures with caution

These range figures are homologated on the Chinese **CLTC** cycle, which is appreciably more favourable than the European WLTP cycle used in France. The observed gap between the two protocols commonly sits between 20 and 25%. A cautious estimate would therefore bring the 822 km CLTC down to an order of 620 to 660 km WLTP, which remains high for a vehicle of this size. The manufacturer also states that its figures come from initial testing on the China-market model.

To place these values against models actually homologated in Europe, our comparison of the [best electric car](/en/blog/best-electric-car/) provides WLTP benchmarks for the French market.

## An interior designed as a lounge {#interior}

This is the model's reason for being, and the bulk of its sales pitch.

The seats in the two rear rows, of **first-class airline** type, combine Nappa leather with 12 cm thick cushions, along with power adjustment and massage functions. A **15.6-inch** ceiling screen, adjustable across five positions, serves as the entertainment hub for rear passengers.

The audio system comprises **20 speakers** by Yamaha. Cabin noise is quoted at **64.5 dB at 120 km/h**, a figure reflecting extensive sound insulation work comparable to that of luxury saloons.

On the electronics side, the 009 carries twin **Snapdragon 8295** chips for its infotainment system, an AI voice assistant, and a perception suite of seven 8-megapixel cameras, four surround-view cameras and a millimetre-wave radar with a 250 m range. Over-the-air updates are supported.

## Is the Zeekr 009 available in France? {#availability-france}

**No.** The 009 is not sold in France, nor more widely in Western Europe. The luxury MPV segment there is too narrow to justify homologation and a commercial rollout.

The brand's European range, launched in France in April 2026, is built around five models: the X urban SUV, the 7X family SUV, the 7GT sports estate, the 001 large estate and the 007 saloon. Details of that range and the launch schedule are covered in our article on [Zeekr in France](/en/blog/zeekr-in-france/).

For premium family use on the French market, two models in the range come closest:

| Model | Type | Price | WLTP range |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Zeekr 7X](/en/blog/zeekr-7x/) | Family SUV | from €52,990 | up to 615 km |
| [Zeekr 001](/en/blog/zeekr-001/) | Large estate | higher positioning | per version |

The [Zeekr 7X](/en/blog/zeekr-7x/) is closest to the 009 in family purpose and equipment level, with the decisive advantage of an 800-volt architecture allowing 10 to 80% charging in 13 minutes. The [Zeekr 001 large estate](/en/blog/zeekr-001/) favours statement presence and cabin length.

On pricing, the 009 trades at around €65,000 equivalent in its home market. Transposing that figure to Europe would be misleading: local taxation and the countervailing duties applied since late 2024 to Chinese electric vehicles imported into the European Union would both need to be added. Our overview of [Chinese SUVs available in France](/en/blog/chinese-suv/) gives a more realistic picture of the prices actually charged.

Buyers in the Paris region looking for a premium family vehicle will find the Mercedes-Benz, smart, Lexus and Geely ranges at [Como, an automotive retailer in Greater Paris](https://como.fr/), with a local network and established after-sales service.
