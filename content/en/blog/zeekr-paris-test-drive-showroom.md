## Where to take a Zeekr test drive around Paris in 2026 {#locations}

A Paris driver has three ways in today, and they do not serve the same purpose.

| Location | Address | Distance from Paris | What you can do there |
|---|---|---|---|
| Como, Saint-Ouen-l'Aumône site | 2 rue Louis Delage, 95310 Saint-Ouen-l'Aumône | about 30 km north-west | test drive, sales advice, ordering |
| Zeekr Roissy Saint-Witz (Bauer Paris) | 67 Grande Rue, 95470 Saint-Witz | about 30 km north | display, test drive, delivery, servicing |
| "Paris and Île-de-France" test drive point | no public address | not applicable | filing an online test drive request |
| Paris Motor Show (12 to 18 October 2026) | Paris Expo, Porte de Versailles, hall 5.2 | Paris 15th | seeing the four models standing still |

The Paris test drive point is not a place you can walk into. It is an entry in the brand's booking tool, with no street or postcode, used to log requests for the Paris region. Our article on [Zeekr Paris and its Paris address](/en/blog/zeekr-paris/) explains how it works.

To get behind the wheel, head for the Val-d'Oise. Both sites sit at a similar distance from the capital, one along the A15, the other along the A1, so the choice mostly depends on where you start from.

## Como, Zeekr distributor in the Val-d'Oise {#como}

The Como group is a **Zeekr** distributor in the Val-d'Oise. It carries the brand at its Saint-Ouen-l'Aumône site, in the Cité de l'automobile business park, where it already represents Geely Auto under the trading name Geely Pontoise.

The trade press reported this location in summer 2026, in its review of the brand's French network:

> "Parmi les nouveaux entrants, signalons l'arrivée [...] du groupe Bauer Paris à Roissy (95), déjà représentant XPeng, et du groupe Como à Pontoise (95), qui distribue aussi le panneau Geely dans cette ville."
> Christophe Bourgeois, [Le Journal de l'Automobile](https://journalauto.com/constructeurs/zeekr-passe-a-la-vitesse-superieure-pour-developper-son-reseau/), 10 July 2026

In plain terms: among the newcomers, Bauer Paris in Roissy and Como in Pontoise, which also sells Geely in that town. The sales team is open Monday to Friday from 8.30am to 7pm, and on Saturdays from 10am to 12pm then 2pm to 6pm. The direct line is +33 1 34 48 38 80, and the page of the [Como dealership in Saint-Ouen-l'Aumône](https://como.fr/pages/nos-concessions/geely-pontoise) gives directions and contacts.

One practical tip: call before you go. As of 27 September 2026 the site does not yet appear in the brand's online test drive tool, and a quick call confirms which demonstrator will be on site on the chosen day.

The group has another advantage for anyone torn between two brands of the same industrial group. With Geely and Zeekr under one roof, both ranges can be driven in a single visit, for instance a Geely SUV under €40,000 against a [Zeekr X](/en/blog/zeekr-x/) of similar size.

## Booking a Zeekr test drive online, step by step {#booking}

The manufacturer's form is short, with four screens.

1. **Pick the model** among those marked as available for a test drive now.
2. **Pick a store or an event** from the network list.
3. **Pick a date and time**: slots only show up once a site is selected, as every store keeps its own calendar.
4. **Enter your details**, stating your profile (private buyer, business or company car driver).

A confirmation email then sums up the appointment, with the site's contact details. To change or cancel, the brand asks you to call the store.

The profile chosen in step 4 matters. A company car driver will have different questions from a private buyer: benefit in kind, long-term lease payments, home charging costs. Stating it at booking lets the advisor prepare a relevant quote.

## What to check during a Zeekr test drive {#checklist}

A test drive goes by fast. Arrive with a short list suited to the model.

### Zeekr X: size in town

The X is 4.43 m long. For a Paris driver, its appeal shows in a tight parallel park and on an underground car park ramp far more than on a fast road. Try the reversing camera and rear visibility, then load the boot with what a weekend away really needs.

### Zeekr 7X: family use and towing

The **Zeekr 7X** is the family pick of the range. It can tow up to 2,000 kg according to the manufacturer, enough for a caravan or a horse box. Bring the whole family along, fit a child seat if possible and check access to the rear bench. Our [Zeekr 7X](/en/blog/zeekr-7x/) review details the three versions.

### Zeekr 7GT: suspension and motorway comfort

The Privilege AWD version of the **Zeekr 7GT** comes with active air suspension, which the two rear-wheel-drive versions lack. That is the point to compare if the site has both. A motorway stretch also reveals road noise, often more telling than acceleration. See our [Zeekr 7GT](/en/blog/zeekr-7gt/) article for the versions.

### Every version: charging and regeneration

Three checks apply to every model. Ask to see the charging screen and the power curve, which drives the 13 to 30 minutes claimed for 10 to 80%. Try the regenerative braking levels on a downhill stretch. And time how long the interface takes to learn, since most settings go through the central screen.
