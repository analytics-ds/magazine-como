Looking for a **Geely dealer** in France is a recent proposition: the Chinese manufacturer only confirmed its first distribution partners in spring 2026. The network is expanding fast, with around thirty sites open by the end of June 2026 and a target of 200 outlets by 2030. Here is where to find an outlet, which groups distribute the brand, and what a Geely dealer can offer today.

> **Key points:**
> - Geely does not sell directly: the brand works through **established automotive retail groups**
> - Around thirty sites by the end of June 2026, 70 expected by year end, 200 by 2030
> - In Greater Paris, the **Como** group covers eight sites, the densest coverage in the region
> - Three models in the French range: Starray EM-i hybrid, E5 electric and EX2 city car
> - A Geely dealer handles sales, test drives, financing and after-sales service

## The Geely network in France in 2026 {#network-france}

Geely Auto confirmed its arrival on the French market on 28 April 2026, announcing its range and its first distribution partners at the same time. The strategy is clear: rather than building a network from scratch, the manufacturer relies on **independent automotive retail groups** that already operate other brands.

This approach has a concrete benefit for buyers. Geely outlets open within established structures, with existing workshops, teams trained in automotive retail and after-sales routines already in place. The new-network risk often held against Chinese newcomers is significantly reduced.

### A fast, quantified rollout

The schedule announced by the brand is precise:

| Milestone | Number of outlets |
|---|---|
| End of June 2026 | 30 sites |
| September 2026 | 50 sites |
| End of 2026 | 70 sites |
| 2027 | 120 sites |
| 2028 | 170 sites |
| 2030 | 200 sites |

That pace places Geely among the fastest rollouts seen recently on the French market, alongside that of [Zeekr, the premium brand from the same group](/en/blog/zeekr-in-france/).

## Official distributors region by region {#distributors-regions}

The first groups selected by Geely Auto France cover complementary territories:

| Distribution group | Cities and departments covered |
|---|---|
| **Como** | Pontoise (95), Vélizy (78), Colombes (92), Fontenay-sous-Bois (94), Paris 12th, Paris 17th, Stains (93), Port Marly (78) |
| **Auto Réal Group** | Bordeaux (33), Toulouse (31), Perpignan (66) |
| **Baldassari Automobiles** | Marseille (13), Toulon (83), Fréjus (83) |
| **Elypse Autos** | Mulhouse (68), Belfort (90), Épinal (88) |
| **BYmyCAR** | Villeneuve-Loubet (06), Le Cannet (06) |
| **Select Auto** | La Rochelle (17) |

Several of these groups already distributed brands from the same technical family. Elypse Autos operated Volvo, Polestar and Lynk & Co, three marques owned by the same shareholder as Geely, which simplifies staff training and parts logistics.

### Areas still to be covered

By mid-2026, coverage remains concentrated in Greater Paris, the South East, the South West and the Grand Est. Normandy, Brittany and the Centre-Val de Loire are still awaiting their first outlets, scheduled for the September and year-end waves. The brand's official locator lists sites as they open, including several addresses in the Hauts-de-France such as Lens, Béthune and Arras.

## The Geely network in Greater Paris {#greater-paris}

Greater Paris is the best served region, and the **Como** group plays a central role there with eight sites:

- **Paris 12th** and **Paris 17th** for the inner city
- **Colombes** (92) and **Fontenay-sous-Bois** (94) in the inner suburbs
- **Stains** (93), **Vélizy** (78), **Port Marly** (78) and **Pontoise** (95) further out

This coverage spans all four corners of the region, following a clear accessibility logic: a buyer in the Paris area is rarely more than thirty minutes from an outlet. The group also distributes Mercedes-Benz, smart and Lexus, giving it a premium service culture that transfers to the Chinese brand. The [offers on new Geely vehicles](https://como.fr/pages/offres-neufs-geely) and test drive bookings are available directly online.

This dual expertise is worth considering. A group used to the premium segment generally applies the same standards of welcome, courtesy vehicles and workshop lead times across all its brands, including the most recent ones. The benchmarks set out in our guide to [choosing a Mercedes dealer](/en/blog/best-mercedes-a-class-dealer/) largely hold true for Geely.

## What a Geely dealer offers {#services}

### Sales and test drives

The dealer is the entry point for discovering the French range, which counts three models:

| Model | Type | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Geely EX2** | Electric city car | from €20,990 |
| **Geely Starray EM-i** | Hybrid compact SUV | €34,990 to €38,990 |
| **Geely E5** | Electric compact SUV | €37,990 to €41,990 |

Test drives can be booked in store and are strongly recommended: on models still unfamiliar to French buyers, handling and perceived interior quality are hard to judge from photographs. Our full profile of the [Geely E5 electric SUV](/en/blog/geely-e5/) and of the [Starray EM-i hybrid](/en/blog/geely-starray/) set out what to examine during the drive.

### Financing

Dealers offer the leasing packages negotiated by the importer. At launch, the brand advertised monthly payments from €299 with a deposit, later settling around €366 to €419 for the Starray EM-i and €399 to €439 for the E5. Full details of these packages are covered in our article on [Geely prices and financing in France](/en/blog/geely-france-price/).

### After-sales service

This is where the network question becomes concrete. A Geely dealer handles routine servicing, the manufacturer warranty and parts orders. The distribution group's ability to share its workshop across several brands directly affects appointment lead times, particularly while the network is still scaling up.

## How to choose your Geely dealer {#choosing}

Three criteria are worth checking before committing:

1. **Distance to the workshop, not just the showroom.** Some outlets open as display space first, with the workshop following later. For servicing, the workshop address is what matters.
2. **How long the group has operated locally.** A long-established distributor has a verifiable service track record, visible through customer reviews of its other brands.
3. **Available stock.** For a brand at launch, delivery times vary widely between sites depending on allocations received. Contacting two or three outlets within the same group often saves several weeks.

The buying process itself is no different from that of an established brand: the steps are detailed in our guide to [buying a Geely in France](/en/blog/buy-a-geely-in-france/). To place the brand in its competitive context, our overview of [Chinese car brands](/en/blog/chinese-car-brands/) provides the relevant reference points.
