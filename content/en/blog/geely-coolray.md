The **Geely Coolray** is one of the Chinese manufacturer's most widely distributed SUVs, with a commercial career that began in 2018 and a presence across around ten markets. Yet this B-segment urban SUV, 4.33 m long and powered by turbocharged petrol engines from 136 to 181 hp, is not sold in France. Here are its full specifications, the markets where it is available, and the Geely models that occupy an equivalent position in the French range.

> **Key points:**
> - The **Geely Coolray** is a 4.33 m urban SUV on the BMA platform, in production since August 2018
> - Turbocharged petrol engines: 1.0 of 136 hp, 1.5 of 177 to 181 hp, plus a 258 hp plug-in hybrid version
> - 330 L boot expanding to 1,038 L, front-wheel drive
> - **Not sold in France**: it is available in the Philippines, Vietnam, South Africa and North Africa
> - It is also produced as the Proton X50 in Malaysia and the BelGee X50 in Eastern Europe

## The Coolray, a B-segment urban SUV {#overview}

The **Geely Coolray** is the export version of the Geely Binyue, launched in China in August 2018. It is a B-segment SUV, the category of the Peugeot 2008, Renault Captur and Volkswagen T-Cross, built on the manufacturer's **BMA** platform.

Its international career began in the Philippines in September 2019, where it quickly became one of the best-selling Chinese models. Vietnam followed in March 2025 and South Africa in June 2026, along with several North African markets including Morocco, Tunisia and Algeria.

### One model, several badges

The Coolray is unusual in existing under different names depending on the market:

- **Geely Binyue** in China, its home market
- **Geely Coolray** for export, notably in South East Asia, Africa and North Africa
- **Proton X50** in Malaysia, Proton being a Geely group subsidiary
- **BelGee X50** in Belarus and Russia since 2023

This multiple distribution explains high production volumes, which works in favour of parts availability and the model's industrial maturity.

### Several rounds of updates

The model has been regularly refreshed: a sports appearance pack in 2021, the Binyue Cool variant in 2022 with redesigned bumpers, a trim update in 2023, then the Binyue L in 2024. Used buyers therefore need to identify the model year precisely, as equipment varies noticeably between phases.

## Geely Coolray specifications {#specifications}

### Dimensions and key figures

| Specification | Geely Coolray |
|---|---|
| Length | 4,330 to 4,380 mm |
| Width | 1,800 mm |
| Height | 1,609 mm |
| Wheelbase | 2,600 mm |
| Boot | 330 L, up to 1,038 L |
| Platform | BMA |
| Drivetrain | Front-wheel drive |
| Segment | Urban SUV, B-segment |

The 2,600 mm wheelbase is generous for the category, which directly benefits rear legroom. At 1,609 mm, overall height stays contained and avoids clearance issues in underground car parks.

### Available engines

Depending on the market, the Coolray receives several turbocharged petrol units:

| Engine | Power | Gearbox |
|---|---|---|
| 1.0 turbo 3-cylinder | 136 hp | 6-speed manual or DCT |
| 1.5 turbo 3-cylinder | 177 hp | 7-speed DCT |
| 1.5 turbo 4-cylinder | 181 hp | 7-speed DCT |
| 1.5 turbo plug-in hybrid | 258 hp combined | Automatic |

The most common export configuration is the **1.5 turbo paired with a 7-speed dual-clutch DCT gearbox**, a combination that delivers strong response for an SUV of this size. The plug-in hybrid version, reserved for certain Asian markets, produces 258 hp and 415 Nm of torque for around 62 km of electric range.

### Standard equipment

On higher trims, the Coolray offers an equipment level above its segment average: large central screen, powered tailgate, heated seats and steering wheel, automatic climate control, cruise control, reversing camera and alloy wheels. This generous specification at a contained price is the model's main commercial argument in its markets.

## Is the Coolray available in France? {#availability-france}

**No, the Geely Coolray is not sold in France.** When Geely Auto France confirmed its arrival on the French market in April 2026, the brand built its range around electrified models, in line with European CO2 regulations and local demand.

A turbocharged petrol SUV such as the Coolray, without electrification on its most widely sold versions, does not fit that positioning. It has never been homologated for Western Europe nor tested by Euro NCAP.

### Used and imported units

A few Coolrays nonetheless circulate in France, from individual imports or export vehicles. Listings on used car platforms place these units between **€19,990 and €23,000** depending on mileage and model year. For reference, the new model starts at 72,800 dinars in Tunisia.

This route calls for caution: no manufacturer warranty in France, homologation to be verified case by case, and parts that do not circulate through the French network. For everyday use, buying an officially distributed model remains considerably safer, as our guide to [buying a Geely in France](/en/blog/buy-a-geely-in-france/) sets out.

## Reliability and owner feedback {#reliability}

Reliability is a systematic question on Chinese models. Here the Coolray has an advantage: its age. In production since 2018 and distributed at scale under four different badges, it benefits from a usage track record few recent Chinese models can claim.

Its 1.5 turbo powertrain stems from **technical cooperation between Geely and Volvo**, the Swedish manufacturer having belonged to the same group since 2010. That kinship, also highlighted on the brand's European models, adds to the model's mechanical credibility.

Owner feedback in the markets where it is sold regularly points to two areas of attention: real-world fuel consumption of the 1.5 turbo in urban use, above homologated figures, and the behaviour of the DCT gearbox at low speed, a trait common to this type of transmission. Conversely, equipment levels and interior space consistently draw positive comments.
