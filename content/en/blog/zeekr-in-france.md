## Zeekr, the premium electric brand of the Geely group {#who-is-zeekr}

**Zeekr** is a 100% electric car brand founded in March 2021 by Chinese group Geely Holding. It was created to compete directly with Tesla, NIO and the German premium brands in the high-end electric vehicle segment.

The brand is not an isolated player. Geely Holding, its parent group, is China's largest private carmaker. It has owned Volvo Cars since 2010, along with Polestar, Lotus, Lynk and Co, and co-owns the smart brand with Mercedes-Benz. This industrial backing sets **Zeekr** apart from many other Chinese newcomers on the European market.

### Rapid industrial growth

The brand's first vehicle, the **Zeekr 001** shooting brake, rolled off the production line in October 2021. By June 2025, just 44 months later, Zeekr announced it had produced its 500,000th vehicle. Few carmakers have reached that volume so quickly.

The brand was also listed on the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE) in May 2024 under the ZK ticker. Since February 2025, it has been part of the Zeekr Group holding company, which combines the Zeekr and Lynk and Co brands within the Geely group.

To understand the group's broader strategy on the French market, the article [Geely in France: the Chinese carmaker arrives in 2026](/en/blog/geely-in-france/) covers the parent company's roll-out and its various brands.

## Zeekr's arrival in France in 2026 {#zeekr-in-france}

**Zeekr France** officially opened its order books on April 2, 2026. The brand had started its European expansion in 2023 through the Netherlands and Sweden, before gradually extending its presence to Norway, Denmark, Belgium, Germany and now France.

### A showroom network in major cities

The commercial roll-out relies on showrooms located in six cities at launch:

- Paris
- Lyon
- Rennes
- Aix-en-Provence
- Frejus
- Saint-Etienne

First deliveries began in **May 2026** for the X and 7X models. Customers who ordered a 001 or a 7GT will be delivered from **July 2026**.

## The Zeekr range available in France {#zeekr-range}

The launch range of **Zeekr in France** includes four models, all 100% electric, covering the most dynamic segments of the premium market.

### Zeekr X: the entry-level SUV

The [Zeekr X is the compact SUV](/en/blog/zeekr-x/) that opens the range at €37,990. At 4.43 m long, it offers up to 415 km of WLTP range and fast charging from 10 to 80% in around 18 minutes.

### Zeekr 7X: the high-tech family SUV

The [Zeekr 7X is the brand's family SUV](/en/blog/zeekr-7x/), priced from €52,990. It claims up to 615 km of WLTP range with its 100 kWh battery and ultra-fast charging thanks to its 800-volt architecture.

### Zeekr 001: the flagship

The [Zeekr 001 shooting brake](/en/blog/zeekr-001/) is the brand's original model and flagship. At 4.97 m long, it is priced from €59,990 with a 100 kWh battery and up to 620 km of WLTP range.

### Zeekr 7GT: the sport wagon

The 7GT is a sporty shooting brake positioned at €45,990. It is derived from the 007 sedan sold in China and completes the range between the X and the 7X.

## Technology: SEA platform and 800-volt architecture {#zeekr-technology}

All **Zeekr** models sold in Europe are built on the SEA platform (Sustainable Experience Architecture), developed by Geely since 2017. This modular architecture dedicated to electric vehicles is also used by other group brands, including smart and Volvo for certain models.

### The range's technical strengths

- **800-volt architecture**: it enables very high charging power, going from 10 to 80% in 13 to 18 minutes depending on the model
- **CATL batteries**: Zeekr relies on the world's largest battery manufacturer, as well as its own Golden Battery cells for certain models
- **22 kW AC charging**: standard on most versions, a rare advantage over competitors that often cap at 11 kW
- **Level 2 driver assistance**: lane keeping, autonomous emergency braking and blind spot monitoring as standard

According to French specialist outlet L'Argus, which detailed the French range and prices in 2026, Zeekr positions itself as "a premium Chinese electric carmaker" whose technological offering directly targets German standards (L'Argus, 2026).

## Pricing, warranty and competitive positioning {#pricing-warranty}

The pricing of **Zeekr in France** sits between mainstream brands and established premium players.

### No French EV bonus

An important point for buyers: **Zeekr** vehicles are built in China and do not reach the environmental score required by the French incentive scheme. They are therefore not eligible for the ecological bonus or the CEE premium. The displayed prices are net prices, with no public subsidy available.

### A 10-year or 200,000 km warranty

To compensate for the brand's lack of track record in France and to reassure buyers about reliability, Zeekr offers a **10-year or 200,000 km** manufacturer warranty on its vehicles. It is one of the longest warranties on the market, ahead of Toyota (10 years under maintenance conditions) and far above German standards (2 to 3 years).
