The **price of a Geely car** in France currently ranges from 20,990 to 41,990 euros, depending on whether the buyer targets the entry-level electric city car or the most equipped SUV. Three models make up the range available at dealerships, each with its own pricing grid and financing options. Here is the breakdown of prices for each model, what the brand charges in other markets, and the levers for financing the purchase at the best rate.

## How much does a Geely cost in France? The full pricing grid

Geely's French catalogue counts three models, all positioned on the electrified segment: a fully electric city car, a plug-in hybrid SUV and a fully electric SUV. The table below summarises the catalogue price of each version.

| Model | Powertrain | Entry price | Top-spec price | Leasing from |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Geely EX2** | Electric city car | 20,990 € | depends on trim | current offers |
| **Geely Starray EM-i** | Plug-in hybrid SUV | 34,990 € | 38,990 € | 366 € / month |
| **Geely E5** | Electric SUV | 37,990 € | 41,990 € | 399 € / month |

These prices include import and customs fees, but not the French ecological bonus, which is closed to vehicles built outside the European Union. Every model still comes with the same 8-year or 200,000-kilometre manufacturer warranty, an argument that matters in the total cost of ownership over time. The full breakdown of the city car's trims and financing options is available in our article on the [price of the Geely EX2](/en/blog/geely-ex2-price/).

## The price of the Starray EM-i, the most accessible plug-in hybrid

The **Starray EM-i** positions itself as the entry point into segment C plug-in hybrid SUVs, with a starting price of 34,990 euros that stays below the 35,000 euro mark seen at most equivalent European rivals. Its top trim caps out at 38,990 euros, a gap of 4,000 euros between the two ends of the range.

This price gives access to an electric range of 135 to 136 km on the WLTP cycle and a total combined range exceeding 1,000 kilometres, thanks to a 29.8 kWh battery paired with a combustion engine. For daily urban trips, the all-electric mode covers most needs, while the combustion engine takes over on the motorway without any charging constraint. The full trim-by-trim pricing grid is available in our report on [Geely prices in France](/en/blog/geely-france-price/).

## The price of the E5, the range's fully electric SUV

The **Geely E5** sits at the top of the French range, priced at 37,990 euros in Pro trim and rising to 41,990 euros in Max trim. The 4,000 euro gap between the two versions is justified by richer equipment and a fast-charging capacity raised to 160 kW, against a more limited charging power on the entry trim.

On the technical side, the E5 offers a WLTP range of 470 to 475 km depending on trim, for a length of 4.62 metres that places it at the heart of the compact electric SUV segment. Against rivals such as the Renault Megane E-Tech or the MG S5 EV, the E5's price-to-range ratio is among the strongest selling points pushed by the Geely network.

## Geely prices outside France: Coolray, GX3, Emgrand

The range sold in France only represents part of Geely's global catalogue. In other markets, particularly in North Africa, the brand distributes older or more affordable models that are absent from the French lineup.

| Model | Market | Price observed |
|---|---|---|
| Geely Coolray | Tunisia | from 72,800 TND (≈ 21,000 to 22,000 €) |
| Geely GX3 Pro | Morocco | 165,000 to 175,896 MAD |
| Geely Emgrand | Morocco | 165,000 to 184,800 MAD |

These prices, quoted in local currency, are not directly comparable to a euro budget given exchange rates and local taxation. They mainly show the gap in positioning between markets where Geely targets volume with entry-level models, and the French market where the brand chose to launch exclusively on electrified powertrains. The case of the Coolray, popular elsewhere but absent from French dealerships, is detailed in our article on the [price of the Geely Coolray](/en/blog/geely-coolray-price/).

## Financing a Geely: leasing, hire purchase and monthly payments

Geely Auto France structured its financing offer in partnership with CA Auto Bank, a subsidiary of Credit Agricole specialised in car credit. Three formulas are offered when buying: cash payment, hire purchase with a purchase option, and long-term leasing.

The leasing monthly payments observed, over a 48-month and 40,000-kilometre basis, are as follows:

- **366 € a month** for the Starray EM-i
- **399 € a month** for the E5 in Pro trim
- **419 € a month** for the E5 in Max trim

These amounts generally do not include insurance or maintenance, offered as separate options, and depend on the initial deposit and the annual mileage subscribed. Several additional schemes can reduce the total cost for an eligible buyer, such as the conversion bonus when trading in an old diesel or petrol vehicle, or certain regional incentives for buying an electric vehicle.

## Where to buy a Geely at the best price in the Paris region

Geely's catalogue price is set nationally, but the actual buying conditions, required deposit, delivery time, launch offers, vary from one distributor to another. The French network remains smaller than that of mainstream brands, which makes comparing dealers all the more useful before signing.
