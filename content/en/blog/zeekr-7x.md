## The Zeekr 7X, family SUV at the heart of the range {#presentation}

The **Zeekr 7X** holds the central position in the French range of [Zeekr, Geely's premium electric brand](/en/blog/zeekr-in-france/). Positioned between the X urban SUV and the large 001 shooting brake, it targets the most contested segment of the electric market: family SUVs, dominated by the Tesla Model Y.

Launched in China in 2024 and introduced in Europe in 2025, the **7X** arrives in France with a strong technical argument: an **800-volt** electric architecture giving it some of the shortest charging times on the market, across all brands.

### A size designed for families

At 4,787 mm long and 1,930 mm wide, the **Zeekr 7X** offers a spacious cabin for five occupants and a 539-litre boot. Its long wheelbase, characteristic of the Geely group's SEA platform, directly benefits rear passenger legroom.

### Three versions in the catalogue

The **Zeekr 7X** range is built around three configurations:

- **RWD 75 kWh**: rear motor of around 416 hp, 75 kWh battery, 480 km WLTP. This is the entry version
- **RWD Long Range 100 kWh**: same motor, battery upgraded to 100 kWh for 615 km WLTP
- **All-wheel drive (AWD)**: two motors for a combined 646 hp, 100 kWh battery, 543 km WLTP

The certified average consumption stands at around 17.7 kWh/100 km in the mixed WLTP cycle, a fair value for an SUV of this size and power.

## Range and ultra-fast charging {#range-charging}

### Up to 615 km of WLTP range

The Long Range version of the **Zeekr 7X** claims **615 km** of WLTP range with its 100 kWh battery. Early European tests indicate it stays above 500 km in moderate winter conditions, a solid result for the category.

### The fastest charging in the segment

This is the model's number one selling point. Thanks to the **800-volt** architecture and high-performance batteries, the **Zeekr 7X** posts record charging times:

- 75 kWh version: 10 to 80% in **13 minutes**
- 100 kWh version: 10 to 80% in **16 minutes**

By comparison, a Tesla Model Y needs around 27 minutes for the same operation, and most European electric SUVs between 28 and 35 minutes.

> "Going from 10 to 80% takes 13 minutes for the Core version and 16 minutes for the 100 kWh versions with a compatible charger."
> — Automobile Propre, Zeekr 7X review, 2026

The standard **22 kW** onboard AC charger completes the package for charging on slower public charging points: a full charge takes a few hours where a standard 11 kW charger needs twice as long.

## Prices and versions available in France {#prices-versions}

### 2026 price list

The **price of the Zeekr 7X** starts at **€52,990** in France:

| Version | Battery | WLTP range | Indicative price |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7X RWD | 75 kWh | 480 km | €52,990 |
| 7X Long Range | 100 kWh | 615 km | around €57,000 |
| 7X AWD Performance | 100 kWh | 543 km | around €60,000 |

Orders have been open since April 2, 2026 and deliveries started in **May 2026**, making the 7X one of the brand's first two models delivered in France along with the [Zeekr X compact SUV](/en/blog/zeekr-x/).

### 10-year warranty, no EV bonus

Like all the brand's models, the **Zeekr 7X** is built in China and does not benefit from any purchase incentive in France. The **10-year or 200,000 km** manufacturer warranty and the comprehensive standard equipment (level 2 autonomous driving, 360° cameras, heated and ventilated seats) are the counterparts put forward by the carmaker.

The **Zeekr 7X** goes head-to-head with the segment's best-seller:

| Model | Starting price | Max WLTP range | 10-80% charging | Max power |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Zeekr 7X** | **€52,990** | **615 km** | **13 to 16 min** | **646 hp** |
| Tesla Model Y | €44,990 | 622 km | 27 min | 514 hp |
| Skoda Enyaq | €46,500 | 587 km | 28 min | 340 hp |

The Tesla Model Y keeps a price advantage and a proprietary charging network, but the **7X** beats it on charging speed, available power and standard equipment. The 10-year warranty also works in the Chinese model's favour.

For a tighter budget, the [Zeekr X from €37,990](/en/blog/zeekr-x/) offers the essentials of the brand's technology in an urban format. Conversely, the [Zeekr 001 shooting brake](/en/blog/zeekr-001/) moves upmarket with nearly 5 m of length and a more prestigious presentation.
