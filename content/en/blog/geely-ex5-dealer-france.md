Looking for a **Geely EX5 dealer France** runs into a wall fairly quickly: the electric SUV is genuinely on sale in France, yet no distributor lists it under that name. In France the EX5 is called the E5. Same car, same platform, same battery, two names depending on the market.

The second obstacle is more ordinary. Geely Auto does not sell directly and hands its entire distribution to independent groups, whose network is still being built. Around ten operators have been announced since the brand launched in late April 2026, for 38 confirmed addresses and a target of 70 sales points by year end.

> **In short:**
> 1. The **Geely EX5** is sold in France as the **E5**, from **37,990 €** to **41,990 €** in Pro+ trim, with 475 km of WLTP range and a 68.4 kWh LFP battery
> 2. Around ten groups distribute it: **Como** (8 sites), **Maurin** (7), **BYmyCAR** (6), **Amplitude** (5), **Auto Réal**, **Baldassari Automobiles** and **Elypse Autos** (3 each), **Select Auto**, **Schumpp Automobiles** and **Moretto** (1 each)
> 3. **Como** is the only distributor based in the **Greater Paris area** and the densest network in the country with its eight dealerships
> 4. The E5 qualifies for **no ecological bonus**, its environmental score sitting below the 60 out of 80 threshold. Every price lever therefore shifts to the dealer: trade-in, a launch lease at **299 € per month** and allocation lead times

## The Geely EX5 is sold as the E5 in France {#name}

The dual naming comes from homologation. The model passed the European Euro NCAP tests under the EX5 reference, earning five stars, while the version marketed in France carries the E5 name. French catalogues, order forms and registration documents all use the latter.

Behind both names sits a single powertrain, developed on the GEA platform (Geely Intelligent Architecture) dedicated to electric vehicles. The full breakdown appears in our [Geely E5 specs and price](/en/blog/geely-e5-electric-suv/) file, but the figures worth memorising before a dealership appointment fit on one line: 4,615 mm long, a 160 kW motor rated at 218 hp, 0 to 100 km/h in 6.9 seconds, 475 km of WLTP range and a stated consumption of 16 kWh per 100 km.

Watch out for a mix-up with another model in the French line-up. The [Geely EX2](/en/blog/geely-ex2/) is an electric city car starting at 20,990 €, unrelated to the SUV in size or in price. Asking for an EX5 at a dealership therefore invites two consecutive misunderstandings, first on the name, then on the segment.

## With no ecological bonus, the dealer becomes the only price lever {#price}

The E5 opens no entitlement to state aid. The 2026 ecological bonus demands an environmental score of at least 60 points out of 80, calculated by Ademe across the vehicle's whole life cycle, battery manufacturing and transport included. An SUV assembled in China falls below that threshold whatever its performance in use.

The scheme's two other conditions are met, with a price under 47,000 € including tax and a mass below 2,400 kg. The barrier is geographic, not financial.

That zero changes how the car is bought. On an eligible model, the subsidy flattens the gaps between distributors. Here there is no cushion: the discount, the trade-in value and the monthly payment come entirely out of the negotiation with the dealer.

That 299 € figure deserves a close reading. It assumes a 4,900 € first payment, close to 13 % of the list price, and a 40,000 km ceiling over four years, meaning 10,000 km a year. Beyond that, the cost per additional kilometre is negotiated site by site.

## Como, the only Geely network in the Greater Paris area {#como}

Eight dealerships, all in the Paris region: Paris 12th, Paris 17th, Colombes, Fontenay-sous-Bois, Stains, Vélizy, Port-Marly and Pontoise. No other Geely distributor operates in the region, which makes Como the mandatory stop for a Paris buyer, and the country's largest network by number of sites ahead of Maurin and its seven openings.

The group also runs 16 Mercedes-Benz, smart and Lexus outlets, with more than 500 staff and around 8,500 vehicles delivered a year. Prices and test drives for the Geely E5 are detailed on [the Como group website](https://como.fr/pages/geely-e5).

One detail slips past every distributor comparison, including our own on the [best Geely dealer](/en/blog/best-geely-dealer/). smart has been a 50/50 joint venture between Mercedes-Benz Group and Geely Holding since 2019, and its recent models sit on an electric platform engineered by Geely. Como's four smart dealerships have therefore been servicing fully electric vehicles built on the engineering of the group that produces the E5 for several years.

It is the only such case among Paris-region distributors, and one of only two across the national network alongside Elypse Autos, which already retailed Volvo, Polestar and Lynk & Co.

## High-voltage certification, the criterion nobody checks {#workshop}

Working on the battery of an electric vehicle is not ordinary mechanical work. The French NF C18-550 standard, recommended by the decree of 20 November 2017, requires a named certification for any technician called to work on a high-voltage circuit, and the legal basis sits in articles R4141-13, R4141-14 and R4544-3 of the French Labour Code.

> "Everyone working on hybrid and electric vehicles falls under NF C18-550 electrical certification: mechanics, body repairers, maintenance technicians, breakdown and recovery operators."
> NF C18-550 training framework, 2025

The levels are not equivalent. B0L covers non-electrical work and takes a single day. B2VL, BRL and BCL, which cover live work, interventions and isolation of the circuit, require three days of initial training and a refresher every three years.

Here is the sticking point: no distributor publishes the number of B2VL-certified technicians per site. Across a network where half the addresses opened between June and August 2026, the gap between a workshop seasoned on electric vehicles and a brand new site appears nowhere. The question therefore has to be asked out loud before signing, not at the first service visit.

A simple wording does the job: how many high-voltage certified technicians work at this site, and if the answer is none, which workshop in the group will the car go to for a battery diagnosis.

## Charging, V2L and test drive: three checks to run at the dealership {#testdrive}

The first check concerns the charger. The E5 accepts fast charging able to go from 30 % to 80 % in 20 minutes, but that performance requires a DC charger of at least 150 kW. A site fitted with a plain 11 kW AC wallbox, on which a full charge takes around eight hours from a 7 kW supply, allows neither a check of the charging curve nor delivery of a vehicle at 100 %.

The second check is real-world range. The 475 km WLTP figure corresponds to 16 kWh per 100 km, a value reachable on a mixed cycle and rarely on the motorway. An hour-long test drive on a dual carriageway with the consumption readout open says more than the spec sheet. For mostly motorway use, the plug-in hybrid in the line-up remains a serious alternative, covered in our file on the [Geely Starray EM-i](/en/blog/geely-starray/).

The third check is the least requested. The E5 carries a V2L function, which powers an external appliance from the vehicle battery. The adapter is not always supplied as standard and few salespeople demonstrate it unprompted. An explicit request during the test drive settles both its presence and the power available.

That leaves lead times. Demonstration vehicles are limited to one or two per site during the first months, and new vehicle allocations vary from one distributor to another. Price and availability benchmarks by model are gathered in our guide to [buying a Geely in France](/en/blog/buy-a-geely-in-france/).
