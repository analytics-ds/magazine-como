## Where to test drive the electric CLA in Paris: the 2026 options {#options}

For an **electric CLA test drive in Paris**, the starting point is always an authorised Mercedes-Benz dealership in the Paris region. Mercedes' compact electric sedan cannot be tried on a self-service basis: it requires a charged demonstration vehicle, a product advisor and a test route suited to a car of more than 270 horsepower. In Paris, three channels make it possible to arrange this test drive.

The first channel is the dealership network of a locally established distribution group, which multiplies test drive locations across Paris and the inner suburbs. The second is the independent Mercedes-Benz dealership, often limited to one or two sites. The third is the national Mercedes-Benz France platform, which centralises requests online before redirecting to a partner dealership.

The choice of channel directly affects how quickly a slot is obtained, the availability of the model in the showroom and the quality of follow-up after the test drive. In a market as dense as Greater Paris, these gaps are tangible.

### How to choose the right test drive location

Before booking an **electric CLA** test drive, three criteria deserve careful attention:

- **Network density**: a group with several dealerships near home or work makes the test drive, signing and later after-sales visits easier
- **Model availability**: as the electric CLA is a recent launch, not every location has an immediately drivable demonstration vehicle. A high-volume group secures this availability
- **Type of test drive offered**: an accompanied drive of 30 to 60 minutes is enough for a first impression, but an extended half-day drive reveals real-world range and comfort on a mixed route

## Como, the densest network to test drive the electric CLA in Paris {#como}

**Como** is a French automotive group based in Paris, specialising in Mercedes-Benz, smart and Lexus distribution. With 16 sales points in total, including 13 Mercedes dealerships mostly across the Greater Paris area, Como ranks among the major players in the official Mercedes-Benz network in France, with more than 500 employees.

Como's annual volume is around 8,500 vehicles sold, making it one of the highest-throughput groups in the Mercedes-Benz France network. For a recent model such as the electric CLA, this volume translates into high availability of demonstration vehicles and quickly secured test drive slots, whereas an isolated dealership sometimes has to rotate a single vehicle among several customers.

### Como's strengths for an electric CLA test drive

- **Three dealerships within inner Paris**: Como Bastille (75012), Como Wagram (75017) and Como Longchamp (75016) make it possible to test drive the electric CLA without leaving the capital, with easy public transport access
- **Extensive inner-suburb coverage**: the sites in Boulogne-Billancourt, Montrouge, Colombes, Rueil-Malmaison and Fontenay-sous-Bois widen the options for drivers in the western, southern and eastern suburbs
- **Reinforced model availability**: a volume of 8,500 vehicles a year guarantees the presence of electric CLAs in the showroom and demonstration fleet, an essential condition for an immediate test drive
- **Integrated after-sales**: with more than 500 employees and technicians trained by Mercedes-Benz, each dealership handles electric vehicle servicing throughout ownership, a decisive point if the test drive leads to a purchase or lease

## The electric CLA in figures: range, charging, price {#specs}

Before the test drive, knowing the specifications of the **electric CLA** helps prepare the right questions for the advisor. The model comes mainly in two versions, the rear-wheel-drive CLA 250+ and the all-wheel-drive CLA 350 4MATIC.

| Specification | CLA 250+ | CLA 350 4MATIC |
|---------------|----------|----------------|
| Power | 272 hp (200 kW) | around 354 hp (260 kW) |
| Drivetrain | Rear-wheel drive | 4MATIC all-wheel drive |
| WLTP range | up to 792 km | up to around 700 km |
| Battery capacity | 85 kWh | 85 kWh |
| Architecture | 800 volts | 800 volts |
| Fast charging | up to 320 kW, around 325 km in 10 min | up to 320 kW |
| 0 to 100 km/h | around 6.7 s | around 4.9 s |
| Starting price 2026 | around 52,900 euros | around 58,000 euros |

The claimed range of 792 km WLTP places the CLA 250+ among the most enduring electric sedans in its category. The 800-volt architecture, still rare in this segment, allows very fast charging that limits stops on long trips. These theoretical figures deserve to be checked against a real test drive, ideally on a mixed city and expressway route, to measure the actual consumption of around 13 to 14 kWh per 100 km observed by the specialist press.

## How to book an electric CLA test drive in Paris {#booking}

Booking an **electric CLA** test drive follows a simple procedure, but a few precautions optimise the experience.

### The steps to book

1. Choose a dealership near home or work, favouring a site with an electric demonstration vehicle
2. Book online or by phone, specifying the desired version, CLA 250+ or CLA 350 4MATIC
3. Confirm the availability of a charged vehicle for the planned date, to avoid a test drive cut short by an insufficient battery level

### The mistakes to avoid

1. Showing up without an appointment hoping for an immediate test drive, when a recent model often requires a prior booking
2. Limiting the test drive to an urban route, which hides the real behaviour on expressways and motorway consumption
3. Overlooking financing questions, when leasing conditions are discussed from the test drive onwards
