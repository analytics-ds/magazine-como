> 1. The **electric CLA** can be ordered from an authorised Mercedes-Benz dealership, through a broker, via the brand's online configurator or bought as a nearly new used car. Only the authorised network combines a test drive, Mercedes-Benz Financial Services funding and a local workshop.
> 2. Four versions sit on the 2026 French price list: CLA 200 EQ (224 hp, 53,650 euros), CLA 250+ EQ (272 hp, from 54,300 euros), CLA 350 4MATIC EQ (354 hp, from 63,550 euros) and Mercedes-AMG CLA 45 4MATIC+ electric (680 hp, 82,100 euros).
> 3. Quoted range runs from 672 to 791 km on the WLTP combined cycle, and up to 922 km on the urban cycle. The 800-volt architecture takes the battery from 10 to 80 percent in 22 minutes.
> 4. Across Greater Paris, **Como** runs 13 Mercedes-Benz dealerships, three of them inside Paris itself (Bastille, Wagram, Longchamp), for test drives, orders and servicing.

## The 4 channels for buying the electric CLA {#channels}

Four routes lead to the **electric CLA** in France, and they differ sharply on price, lead time and after-sales support. The table below compares them on the criteria that actually decide a purchase above 50,000 euros.

| Criterion | Authorised dealership | Broker | Mercedes configurator | Nearly new used |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Price recorded (CLA 250+) | 54,300 € list | 54,300 € list, discount rare at launch | 54,300 € list | 59,900 to 67,900 € by version |
| Test drive before buying | Yes, by appointment | No | No | Depends on seller |
| Mercedes-Benz Financial Services funding | Yes | No | Yes, routed to the network | Depends on seller |
| Local authorised workshop | Yes | No | Through the network | Depends on seller |
| Lead time after order | 3 to 6 months by version | 3 to 6 months | 3 to 6 months | Immediate |
| Configuration choice | Full | Factory stock only | Full | None |
| **Verdict** | **The reference channel on this model** | Pointless while the discount stays at zero | Useful for pricing, not for signing | For buyers in a hurry |

One point deserves stating up front, because it runs against the usual reflex: on the electric CLA, buying nearly new saves almost nothing. A check carried out on 20 September 2026 at an authorised dealer group in Normandy listed an electric CLA with 50 kilometres on the clock at 67,900 euros, while a new CLA 350 4MATIC AMG Line leaves the price list at 68,300 euros. Four hundred euros apart, for a car already registered, with no configuration choice and several months of warranty gone.

## The electric CLA in 2026: versions, range and prices {#range}

The third-generation CLA has moved to a dedicated electric platform, with two battery sizes and three power levels before the AMG derivative.

| Version | Battery | Power | Drivetrain | List price |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CLA 200 EQ AMG Line | 58 kWh LFP | 224 hp (165 kW) | Rear-wheel drive | 53,650 € |
| CLA 250+ EQ Progressive Line | 85 kWh | 272 hp (200 kW) | Rear-wheel drive | 54,300 € |
| CLA 250+ EQ AMG Line | 85 kWh | 272 hp (200 kW) | Rear-wheel drive | 59,050 € |
| CLA 350 4MATIC EQ Progressive Line | 85 kWh | 354 hp (260 kW) | All-wheel drive | 63,550 € |
| CLA 350 4MATIC EQ AMG Line | 85 kWh | 354 hp (260 kW) | All-wheel drive | 68,300 € |
| Mercedes-AMG CLA 45 4MATIC+ electric | 85 kWh | 680 hp (500 kW) | All-wheel drive | 82,100 € |

The 650-euro gap between the CLA 200 EQ in AMG Line and the CLA 250+ EQ in Progressive Line is the most interesting trade-off in the range. For that sum, the battery grows from 58 to 85 kWh and power rises from 224 to 272 horsepower. The range gain runs into the hundreds of kilometres.

Homologated range spans 672 to 791 km on the WLTP combined cycle depending on version and wheel size, and climbs to between 768 and 922 km on the urban cycle. Quoted consumption sits between 12.2 and 14.7 kWh per 100 km on the combined cycle.

Charging is the model's other technical argument. The electrical architecture runs at 800 volts, which allows a charging power well above the segment average.

> "Thanks to an 800-volt architecture, maximum power reaches 320 kW. Enough to go from 10 to 80 percent charge in just 22 minutes, or recover 325 km of range in 10 minutes."
> Valentin Cimino, [Automobile Propre](https://www.automobile-propre.com/articles/mercedes-cla-electrique-quels-tarifs-pour-la-berline-aux-790-km-dautonomie/), 30 April 2025

Those 22 minutes change the logic of a long trip. On a Paris to Lyon run, a single charging stop is enough, and it lasts about as long as a quick lunch. Daily charging cost depends mostly on the method used, and the tariff breakdown sits in our comparison of [electric car charging prices](/en/blog/electric-car-charging-price/).

## Buying from an authorised Mercedes-Benz dealership {#dealership}

The authorised dealership remains the channel that concentrates the most functions in one place: the test drive, the order, the funding and the servicing.

A test drive matters more on this model than on a petrol hatchback. The electric CLA measures 4.72 metres long and 1.47 metres tall, with a sloping roofline that changes rear-seat access and three-quarter rear visibility. Two things judged in five minutes behind the wheel, never on a spec sheet.

Funding is the second argument. Mercedes-Benz Financial Services, the manufacturer's finance arm, handles lease purchase, contract hire and conventional credit within a single monthly payment that can include servicing and extended warranty. Those terms are only reachable through the authorised network. For an electric CLA on a lease, launch payments recorded at dealerships start around 545 to 595 euros per month depending on version and deposit.

The third argument is after-sales. An 85 kWh electric saloon running at 800 volts is serviced and updated by a workshop trained on that architecture. The distance between home and that workshop therefore counts as much as the discount obtained at signing, over a three to four-year ownership period.

## Como, the densest authorised network in Greater Paris {#como}

Across Greater Paris, [Como](https://como.fr/pages/nouveau-mercedes-benz-cla) runs 13 Mercedes-Benz dealerships, three of them inside Paris itself: Bastille in the 12th, Wagram in the 17th and Longchamp in the 16th. The rest of the network covers the inner and outer suburbs, with Boulogne, Porte d'Orléans, Colombes, Stains, Fontenay, Bonneuil, Port-Marly, Boulogne-Reine and the Val-d'Oise.

The group sells around 8,500 vehicles a year and employs more than 500 staff across the Mercedes-Benz, smart, Geely and Lexus brands. That density changes one concrete thing for a Paris-region buyer: the odds that an electric CLA demonstrator sits less than twenty minutes away on a Saturday morning.

Test drives are booked online on the model page, then at the chosen site. Our dedicated guide covers the available locations and the slots worth targeting for an [electric CLA test drive in Paris](/en/blog/where-to-test-drive-electric-cla-paris/).

## Broker, configurator and used cars: what they are really worth {#other-channels}

### The broker, with no leverage on a launch model

A car broker earns its place through a discount negotiated on factory stock volumes. On a model launched less than eighteen months ago, with demand above production, that discount shrinks to almost nothing. The electric CLA falls into that category in 2026. A broker can therefore quote the list price, with no test drive and no authorised workshop attached.

### The Mercedes configurator, useful for pricing

The Mercedes-Benz online configurator builds a version, shows the total price with options and generates a configuration reference. It does not close the sale: the order moves to a dealership chosen by the buyer. It is a good preparation tool before an appointment, not a standalone purchase channel.

### Nearly new, rarely the better maths

The first electric CLAs reaching the used market carry very low mileage, between 50 and 9,300 km, at prices between 59,900 and 67,900 euros depending on body style and trim. Measured against the new list price, those figures leave a thin saving against what the buyer gives up: bespoke configuration, full warranty and first ownership.

Used regains its logic on one criterion alone, the wait. A car in stock is delivered within days, while a factory order takes three to six months. When the need is urgent, the price gap becomes secondary.

## What budget to plan for each purchase method {#budget}

Three purchase methods coexist, with different cost logics.

| Method | Upfront commitment | Indicative monthly cost | Ownership at term |
|---|---|---|---|
| Outright purchase | 53,650 to 82,100 € | None | Yes, immediately |
| Lease purchase (LOA) | Deposit of 10 to 20 percent | 545 to 595 €/month at entry level | Buyout option |
| Contract hire (LLD) | Adjustable deposit | 545 to 700 €/month by mileage | No, car returned |

The calculation to run before signing is not the monthly payment on its own, but the total cost over the contract, deposit and end-of-term reconditioning charges included. A lower payment tied to 10,000 km a year costs more than a higher payment at 20,000 km as soon as real use exceeds the allowance.

Running an electric car trims part of the bill: no oil change, no timing belt, no air filter, and brake pads that last longer thanks to energy recovery. What remains is electricity consumption, between 12.2 and 14.7 kWh per 100 km, or roughly 2.50 to 3 euros per 100 km on home charging at off-peak rates.

## Where to buy the electric CLA based on your profile {#profiles}

**Paris-region driver who wants a test drive before signing.** The local authorised network is the only route that combines a test drive, bespoke configuration and a workshop less than twenty minutes away. Three Paris addresses at Como make the appointment fit inside a lunch break.

**Driver on contract hire for business use.** Contract hire through Mercedes-Benz Financial Services, arranged at a dealership, folds servicing and extended warranty into a single payment. The mileage declared has to match real use, not an optimistic estimate.

**Buyer in a hurry with no configuration requirement.** Nearly new delivers within days. Check the service history, the odometer reading and the first registration date, then compare against the list price of the equivalent version before closing.

**Buyer chasing the biggest discount.** This profile will come up short on this model in 2026. On an electric saloon early in its commercial cycle, the negotiating lever sits on included options and the trade-in value of the old car, not on the base price.
