The **Geely EX2** marks the arrival of the Chinese brand Geely's electric city car on the French market. Priced from €20,990, it targets urban drivers looking for an affordable, well-equipped electric vehicle that remains versatile enough for daily use. Positioning, range, price and equipment: here is everything you need to know about this new model.

> **In brief:**
> - The **Geely EX2** is a 100% electric B-segment city car, 4.13 m long
> - Priced in France from €20,990 (Pro version), before ecological bonus
> - WLTP range of roughly 225 to 325 km depending on version, or 280 to 320 km in real use
> - Motor up to 114 hp, LFP battery, fast charging from 30 to 80% in 21 to 25 minutes
> - Built by the Geely group, also known as the Xingyuan or Geometry E

## What is the Geely EX2? {#overview}

The **Geely EX2** is a fully electric B-segment city car measuring 4.13 metres long. It belongs to the Geely group, a Chinese automotive giant that also owns Volvo, Polestar, Zeekr and Lynk and Co, and co-owns smart with Mercedes-Benz. The model is based on the GEA platform (Global Intelligent New Energy Architecture), dedicated to the group's electrified vehicles.

Depending on the market, this vehicle is also sold as the Geely Xingyuan in China or the Geometry E. In France, it is distributed under the EX2 name, alongside the [Geely E5 electric SUV](/en/blog/geely-e5-electric-suv/) that supports the brand's rollout. To understand the manufacturer's overall strategy, our feature on [Geely in France](/en/blog/geely-in-france/) details its arrival and line-up.

## Design and dimensions {#design}

At 4,135 mm long with a 2,650 mm wheelbase, the **Geely EX2** offers a generous cabin for a city car. The exterior styling remains understated and modern, with rounded lines and the closed front end typical of electric vehicles.

In terms of practicality, the rear boot offers 375 litres, a solid figure for the category, complemented by a front trunk (frunk) of around 70 litres, ideal for storing charging cables.

## Range and battery {#range}

The **Geely EX2** is offered with two battery capacities, both using LFP (lithium iron phosphate) technology, known for its durability and safety:

- a battery of around 35.4 kWh on entry versions
- a battery of around 47 kWh on higher versions

The WLTP homologated range spans roughly 225 to 325 km depending on the version. In real conditions, early tests report a range of 280 to 320 km in mixed use, consistent with urban and suburban driving.

## Performance and charging {#performance}

The powertrain delivers up to 114 horsepower (about 83 kW) and 150 Nm of torque on the best-equipped versions, with an entry version around 78 to 82 horsepower. The 0 to 100 km/h sprint takes 9.2 to 11.5 seconds depending on power, and top speed is limited to 130 km/h.

The city car accepts DC fast charging up to 80 kW, allowing a 30 to 80% top-up in 21 to 25 minutes. On a 7.4 kW AC charger, a full charge takes about 5 hours, ideal for overnight home charging.

## Equipment and technology {#equipment}

Despite its aggressive pricing, the **Geely EX2** offers a full equipment level from the entry version, with a large central touchscreen, a digital instrument cluster, air conditioning and a set of driver aids. The equipment grows richer on the Max and Ultra versions.

## Price and versions {#price}

In France, the **Geely EX2** comes in three trims:

| Version | Price (before bonus) |
|---|---|
| Pro | €20,990 |
| Max | €23,490 |
| Ultra | €25,490 |
