## Why the choice of dealer matters

A Mercedes-Benz dealer is not just a point of sale. It is a long-term partner for servicing, warranties and vehicle follow-up. A poor choice can lead to extra costs and a disappointing experience.

## Selection criteria

### Authorisation and network size

A dealer authorised by Mercedes-Benz France meets the manufacturer's standards on team training, tooling and genuine parts. Large groups such as Como run [several outlets across the Paris region](/en/blog/best-mercedes-dealers-paris/), which makes aftersales easier to access.

### Stock and availability

Wide stock in new and [certified used](/en/blog/best-used-mercedes-dealer-paris/) cars lets you compare models, trims and prices. It avoids ordering blind and having to wait several months.

### Aftersales

Aftersales is the most important criterion over time. Check:

- Appointment availability (average waiting time)
- Whether a courtesy car is provided
- Opening hours (some allow evening drop-off)
- How transparent the repair quotes are

### Financing

[Leasing terms](/en/blog/best-mercedes-leasing-dealer-paris/) vary from one dealer to another. Compare rates, required deposits and the services included.

### Customer reviews

Check Google reviews, forums and social media. A dealer rated above 4/5 across more than 200 reviews is generally reliable.

## Questions to ask on your first visit

- What is the delivery lead time for the model I want?
- What are the terms for taking my current car in part-exchange?
- Do you offer extended test drives (24 hours or a weekend)?
- What do your service packs cost?
- Is the [electric vehicle](/en/blog/best-electric-car/) available to test drive?

## Verdict

Take the time to visit at least two dealerships and compare written offers. The ideal dealer combines technical expertise, competitive commercial terms and responsive customer service.
