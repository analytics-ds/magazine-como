## The Zeekr 001, the brand's founding model {#presentation}

The **Zeekr 001** is not just another model in the range: it is the car that launched the brand. Unveiled in April 2021 and in production since October 2021, it was the first vehicle from [Zeekr, Geely's premium electric brand](/en/blog/zeekr-in-france/), and remains its brand image.

Its format is unusual on the market: a **shooting brake** measuring 4.97 m long, halfway between a traditional wagon and a raised coupe. The 3 m wheelbase, one of the longest in the category, frees up generous interior space, especially in the rear seats.

### A model already proven in Europe

Unlike other Chinese newcomers, the **Zeekr 001** is not arriving in unknown territory. It has been sold in the Netherlands and Sweden since 2023, then in several other European markets. The version sold in France in 2026 therefore benefits from several years of European customer feedback and successive model updates.

The carmaker produced its 500,000th vehicle in June 2025 across all ranges, demonstrating an already established industrial pace.

### Powertrains: from 272 hp to 544 hp

The **Zeekr 001** range is built around two motor architectures:

- **Rear-wheel drive (RWD)**: a 200 kW electric motor, or 272 hp, on the rear axle. This is the range-focused version
- **All-wheel drive (AWD)**: two electric motors for a combined output of 400 kW, or 544 hp. The 0 to 100 km/h sprint takes 3.8 seconds

This performance puts the top version on par with the segment's sports references, such as the Tesla Model S or the entry-level Porsche Taycan, at a significantly lower price.

## Range and charging {#range-charging}

### A 100 kWh battery across the range

All European versions of the **Zeekr 001** carry a **100 kWh** battery supplied by CATL, the world's largest manufacturer. The certified WLTP range goes from 580 km for the Performance AWD version up to **620 km** for the Long Range rear-wheel drive version.

In real-world use, European tests report ranges between 450 and 550 km depending on conditions, placing the model among the longest-range electric cars on the market.

### Charging: 30 minutes from 10 to 80%

The **Zeekr 001** accepts a maximum DC charging power of 200 kW. On a suitable fast charger, going from 10 to 80% battery takes about **30 minutes**.

The **22 kW** onboard AC charger is another notable asset: it allows a full charge in around 5.5 hours on a public AC charging point, where most competitors cap at 11 kW and take twice as long.

## Prices and versions available in France {#prices-versions}

### 2026 price list

The **price of the Zeekr 001** in France starts at **€59,990** for the entry-level rear-wheel drive version. Higher versions, with extended range or all-wheel drive, range between €65,000 and €72,000 depending on configurations and options.

| Version | Drivetrain | Power | Indicative price |
|---|---|---|---|
| 001 entry level | RWD | 272 hp | €59,990 |
| 001 Long Range | RWD | 272 hp | around €65,000 |
| 001 Performance AWD | AWD | 544 hp | around €72,000 |

Orders have been open since April 2, 2026 and the first French deliveries are scheduled from **July 2026**.

### No EV bonus, but a 10-year warranty

Built in China, the **Zeekr 001** is not eligible for the French ecological bonus or the CEE premium. In return, the brand covers the vehicle with a **10-year or 200,000 km** manufacturer warranty, one of the longest on the market.
